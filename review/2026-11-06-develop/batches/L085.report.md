## Flags

### Suspected factual errors in existing text
- plane-electromagnetic-waves.md, Core Idea: "their magnitudes are equal in SI units". In SI, |B| = |E|/c (the file's own Explainer says so). Magnitudes are equal in Gaussian units.
- plane-waves-in-vacuum.md, Explainer: "S = E × H/μ₀" should be E × H or E × B/μ₀. Also "|S| = |E|²/(μ₀c) = c²ε₀|E|²". The correct form is ε₀c|E|², not c²ε₀|E|².
- polarization-of-waves.md, question 0 explanation: "A moment later: E_x decreases, E_y becomes negative — the vector rotates clockwise ... viewed from the +z direction". At z = 0, E_y = E₀cos(−ωt + π/2) = +E₀ sin ωt, which becomes positive. The rotation is counterclockwise as seen facing the oncoming wave. The answer "left-circular" (optics convention) holds, but the reasoning is wrong.
- poynting-vector-energy-flow.md, question 3 explanation: "A radially outward E would give a Poynting vector ... circumferential". r̂ × φ̂ = ẑ, so it would point along the axis, not around it. Explainer: "electromagnetic momentum flux density is S/c²". S/c² is the momentum density. The momentum flux is the Maxwell stress tensor.
- radiation-accelerating-charges.md, question 3 and Explainer: the classical atomic-collapse problem is called the "ultraviolet catastrophe". That term refers to blackbody radiation. Explainer also says "the dependence on 1/c³ makes radiation a relativistic effect", which is misleading.
- radiation-damping-radiation-reaction.md, Explainer: "F_damp ∝ −ωv". For sinusoidal motion ȧ = −ω²v, so the force is ∝ −ω²v. The lifetime sentence "τ ≈ r_e/c ~ 10⁻²³ s times (λ/a)² ~ 10⁻⁸ s" is garbled.
- radiation-reaction-force.md, Explainer: "The full equation of motion is then m ȧ⃗ = F_external + F_rad". It should be m a = F_ext + F_rad. Core Idea "opposing acceleration" is misleading: the force is along ȧ, not opposite a.
- rectangular-waveguide-propagation.md, Explainer: "c/(2a) < f < c/a (or c/(2b) if b < a/2)". The upper limit becomes c/(2b) when b > a/2, not b < a/2.
- refractive-index-and-dispersion.md, Explainer: "Far above resonance ... the refractive index approaches 1 from below and eventually n < 1 near resonance". The order is garbled: n < 1 just above resonance and approaches 1 from below far above it. Question 1 explanation attributes pulse spreading to different phase velocities. Spreading comes from group-velocity dispersion.
- relativistic-particle-em-coupling.md, question 0 explanation: "kinetic momentum changes even though there is no classical force". With E = B = 0, d(mγv)/dt = 0, so kinetic momentum is conserved. The stated answer gets the physics backwards. Explainer: "The non-relativistic Lorentz force F = q(E + v × B) ... breaks Lorentz symmetry". With p = γmv, that force law is exactly relativistic. Also "dp⁰/dτ = γ dE/dt" is missing a factor of 1/c.
- resonant-cavities-em.md, question 0 explanation: "there is no partial resonance at nearby off-mode frequencies". A finite-Q cavity responds over a Lorentzian of width about f₀/Q. Also, 3.1 GHz could match a higher mode. Explainer microwave-oven sentence ("frequency matched to the cavity") is doubtful: oven cavities are multimode and not tuned to the magnetron.
- retarded-potentials.md, Explainer: "By differentiating these integrals to get E and B, you arrive at the Liénard-Wiechert potentials". The LW potentials come from evaluating the retarded integrals for a point charge. Differentiating gives the fields.
- separation-variables-elliptic-equations.md, Core Idea lists "wave equations" among elliptic PDEs. The wave equation is hyperbolic. P_ℓ^m are associated Legendre functions, not Legendre polynomials.
- synchrotron-radiation.md, Explainer and question 1: the non-relativistic pattern is described as "isotropic (a donut shape)". A sin²θ donut is not isotropic. Question 0 explanation says LEP losses per revolution "were in the MeV range". At about 100 GeV, U₀ ≈ 88.5 keV × E⁴[GeV]/ρ[m] gives losses in the GeV range.
- transverse-electric-modes.md, Explainer and question 0: below cutoff the mode "decays within a few skin depths". The evanescent decay length 1/γ is set by geometry and frequency. It is unrelated to the skin depth.
- radiation-from-accelerated-charges.md, Explainer: "the near-field Poynting vector goes as 1/r⁴ after integration". It is 1/r⁴ before integration.
- waveguides-transmission-lines.md, Explainer: "boundary conditions require the transverse electric field to vanish at the walls". It should be the tangential E.
- waveguides-propagation-modes.md, question 1 explanation: modal dispersion is attributed to different phase velocities. Arrival times are set by group velocities.
