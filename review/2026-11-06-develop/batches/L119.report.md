### Suspected factual errors / formatting in existing text
- chandler-hammett-hardboiled-innovation.md, Explainer: quoted Big Sleep opening "It was about eleven o'clock in the morning, mid October, with sun in the windows and a view of the Hollywood sign" is misquoted. The actual line continues "with the sun not shining and a look of hard wet rain in the clearness of the foothills."
- alternate-history-divergence.md, How It's Best Learned: "Phillip K. Dick" should be "Philip K. Dick".
- cosmic-horror-insignificance.md, How It's Best Learned: lists Ted Chiang's "Story of Your Life" as cosmic horror. It is a first-contact SF story with a tone of acceptance, not cosmic horror.
- cozy-mystery-amateur-detective.md, How It's Best Learned: cites Martha Grimes as a contemporary cozy author, but her Richard Jury series centers a Scotland Yard detective, not an amateur.
- austen-social-satire-marriage-plot.md, Core Idea: "Jane Austen established the romance plot" overstates. Courtship/marriage-plot novels (e.g., Richardson, Burney) predate her.
- christie-puzzle-plot-fair-play.md, Core Idea: "Christie pioneered the puzzle-plot mystery" overstates. Fair-play puzzle mysteries predate her; she was a leading Golden Age practitioner.
- doyle-holmes-deductive-method.md, Explainer: "Holmes invented the modern detective" ignores Poe's Dupin (1841), which set the eccentric-genius-plus-narrator-friend model Doyle built on.
- detective-fiction-investigation-logic.md, Core Idea: "The genre depends on fair play" is too broad. Fair play is a convention of the puzzle mystery; much detective fiction (hardboiled, many Holmes stories) withholds facts.
- crime-fiction-underworld-perspective.md, Questions item 2 (true-false): "Crime fiction and detective fiction can both feature criminals as protagonists" keyed false. Questionable: "crime fiction" is often an umbrella term that includes detective fiction, and detective fiction sometimes has criminal protagonists.
- fantasy-magic-systems-formal-rules.md, Explainer: "The best magic systems are the most limited, most rule-bound, most costly" overclaims. Soft magic systems (e.g., Tolkien, Earthsea) are widely admired.
- cosmic-horror-insignificance.md, Core Idea: "reality is fundamentally hostile to human understanding" sits uneasily with the file's emphasis on indifference.
