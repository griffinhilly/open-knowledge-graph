The Vroman effect is the time-dependent displacement of early-adsorbed abundant proteins by higher-affinity ones, not the fast adsorption itself.
- domains/chemistry/materials-chemistry/biomaterials-chemistry.md, Explainer: "releasing Ca^2+ and Si^4+ ions". Bioactive glass releases soluble silica (silicic acid, Si(OH)4); free Si4+ ions do not exist in water.
