## Flags

Suspected factual errors in existing text:
- running-coupling-constants.md, Core Idea: "In QED, the coupling increases at higher energies (charge is anti-screened at short distances)". QED screens; anti-screening is the QCD gluon effect. The Explainer of the same file says "screen". I did not repeat the term.
- running-coupling-constants.md, Explainer: the one-loop formula with 2α/(3π) (electron only) "predicts that alpha reaches the value 1/128 at the Z boson mass". Electron-only running gives 1/α ≈ 134.5 at M_Z. Reaching about 128 needs all charged leptons and quarks, including the hadronic contribution.
- running-coupling-constants.md, Explainer: "The remarkable (and experimentally verified) fact is that the three couplings ... approach each other". This is an extrapolation, not an experimental verification.
- loop-diagrams-divergences.md, Q0 correct option: "integrand falls off as 1/k^2 ... diverges logarithmically because 1/k^2 times k^3 = k grows". This is internally inconsistent; it would be a quadratic divergence. The explanation correctly says the integrand goes as 1/k^3.
- loop-diagrams-divergences.md, Explainer: "electron self-energy (fermion loop correction to the electron propagator)". The self-energy has a photon loop on the electron line, not a fermion loop. It also says the vacuum polarization "renormalizes the electric charge" and the vertex "renormalizes the coupling". By Z1 = Z2, the vertex does not contribute to charge renormalization.
- cross-sections-decay-rates.md, Explainer: "dσ/dΩ = |M|^2/(64π^2 s)" is stated generally. It holds only when |p_f| = |p_i| (for example equal masses or massless particles); otherwise there is a factor |p_f|/|p_i|.
- dirac-field-quantization.md and klein-gordon-field-quantization.md: the mode expansion uses the measure d^3p/((2π)^3 2E_p) together with [a_p, a_q†] = (2π)^3 δ^3(p − q). These normalizations are inconsistent. You need either 1/√(2E_p) in the expansion or (2π)^3 2E_p δ^3 in the commutator. Klein-Gordon also attaches a_p to e^{+ipx}, the opposite of the usual convention for a mostly-minus metric.
- electromagnetic-field-quantization.md, Explainer: "timelike and longitudinal photons with negative norm". In Gupta-Bleuler, only the timelike states have negative norm.
- electromagnetic-field-quantization.md, Q3 answer: a photon mass term "would make the theory non-renormalizable in its naive form". An abelian massive vector coupled to a conserved current (massive QED) is renormalizable. The renormalizability problem arises for non-abelian massive vectors.
- feynman-diagrams-systematic.md, Explainer: "perturbation theory converges rapidly for QED". The QED series is believed to be asymptotic, not convergent (Dyson's argument). Low orders decrease rapidly.
- non-abelian-gauge-theories.md, Q1 explanation: "SU(2) has 3 generators (the W+, W-, and Z bosons before electroweak symmetry breaking)". The SU(2) bosons are W1, W2, W3. The Z is a mixture of W3 and B.
- non-abelian-gauge-theories.md, Q2: the item is typed true-false, but its question text is a "Why ...?" question. It is malformed.
- goldstone-theorem-qft.md, Q0 explanation: "The unbroken SU(2) generators correspond to massive modes". Unbroken generators do not correspond to massive modes; they give no Goldstone bosons. Also, in the Q3 answer, φ = (v + ρ)e^{iθ/v} lacks the 1/√2 needed for canonical kinetic terms with |∂φ|².
- confinement-and-hadrons.md, Explainer: "More exotic combinations (tetraquarks, pentaquarks, glueballs) ... have been observed experimentally". Glueballs have candidates but no established observation. Also, "A rigorous analytical proof of confinement ... remains one of the unsolved Millennium Prize Problems": the Millennium problem is Yang-Mills existence and mass gap, not confinement as such. The same imprecision appears in qcd-basics.md Q3.
- cpt-theorem.md, Explainer: "exactly equal masses (tested to parts per trillion for proton/antiproton and electron/positron)". The electron/positron mass equality bound is at roughly the 10^-8 to 10^-9 level. The parts-per-trillion results are for p/p̄ charge-to-mass ratios and e± g-factors. Also, "processes that violate CP ... -- the Sakharov conditions" omits baryon-number violation and departure from equilibrium.
- anomalies-qft.md, Explainer: "Global anomalies (anomalies in global symmetries)". In standard usage, "global anomaly" means a non-perturbative anomaly such as Witten's SU(2) anomaly. Anomalies of global symmetries are usually called 't Hooft or ABJ-type anomalies. The normalization (e^2/16π^2) F F̃ is also off by a factor of 2 if F̃ = (1/2)εF. The standard form is (e^2/8π^2) F F̃ or (e^2/16π^2) εFF.
- effective-field-theory.md, Explainer: "the Standard Model (which contains only dimension-4 operators at leading order)" and new physics "manifests only through tiny corrections of order (E/Lambda)^2". The SM contains a dimension-2 Higgs mass term, and the dimension-5 Weinberg operator gives O(E/Λ) effects (neutrino masses).
- functional-methods-generating-functionals.md, Q2 (true-false, answer true): "the tree-level approximation of Gamma gives the full quantum result" is ambiguous. The tree-level approximation of Γ is S, which does not give the full result. The intended claim is "using the full Γ at tree level".
- spin-statistics-theorem.md, Q1 (answer true): "all states have zero norm". This is overstated; the failure is that the Fock-space norm cannot be positive definite.
- qed-vertex-basic-processes.md, Q0 correct option: "the photon-photon interaction is absent ... because it is not gauge invariant". Gauge-invariant photon self-couplings such as (F^2)^2 exist; they are absent from the renormalizable QED Lagrangian (dimension 8) and arise only through loops.
- asymptotic-freedom.md, Q3 answer: "Asymptotic freedom and confinement are two sides of the same coin: the same beta function ... makes it large at low energies". This overstates the case: the perturbative beta function cannot be trusted where the coupling is large, and confinement is not derived from it.
- qcd-basics.md, Q2 (true-false, answer true): "QCD is an exact copy of QED with U(1) -> SU(3), and all differences follow from this single change". This is overstated: the theories also differ in flavor content, quark masses, the θ term and the coupling values.
