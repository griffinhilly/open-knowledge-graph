## Flags
- viral-replication-dna-polymerase.md, Core Idea: "Viral replication is rapid and often lacks proofreading, leading to high mutation rates and antigenic drift in persistent viruses like herpesviruses and poxviruses." Herpesvirus and poxvirus DNA polymerases have 3'-5' exonuclease proofreading, and these large DNA viruses have low mutation rates. "Antigenic drift" is an influenza term and is not a standard description of herpesvirus or poxvirus evolution. The same claim recurs in the Explainer's last paragraph ("Many viral DNA polymerases lack this proofreading entirely ... drives antigenic drift ... herpesviruses") and in the explanation of question 3 (0-based).
- viral-replication-strategies-rna-vs-dna.md, Core Idea: "replicate in the nucleus using host DNA polymerase and repair machinery (herpesviruses)". Herpesviruses encode their own DNA polymerase (the acyclovir target), as the sibling file viral-replication-dna-polymerase.md says. The Explainer repeats this ("Herpesviruses do exactly this ... co-opt the existing replication ... machinery") and says they "depend on the host cell being in a replication-competent state", which does not fit herpesviruses replicating in non-dividing neurons during reactivation.
- viral-replication-strategies-rna-vs-dna.md, Core Idea: "Reverse-transcribing viruses (retroviruses) uniquely use reverse transcriptase". Hepadnaviruses (hepatitis B) also use reverse transcriptase.
- viral-replication-strategies-rna-vs-dna.md, Explainer: "provirus is then transcribed by normal host RNA polymerase whenever the cell divides". Proviral transcription is not tied to cell division; the provirus is copied with host DNA when the cell divides. Same paragraph: "no single vaccine has eradicated it" implies HIV vaccines exist; no licensed HIV vaccine exists.
- viral-replication-strategies-rna-vs-dna.md, question 2 (true-false, answer true): "All RNA viruses must encode their own RNA-dependent RNA polymerase". Retroviruses carry RNA genomes but do not use an RdRp, and hepatitis delta virus uses host RNA polymerase II. Consider rewording the stem or changing the answer.
- viral-pathogenesis-and-disease.md, Core Idea: "persistent infections (HBV, HIV, herpesviruses) establish latency". HBV persists as chronic infection with cccDNA and ongoing replication, which is not usually called latency. Minor.
- yeast-fermentation-and-metabolic-pathways.md, Explainer and questions 0, 1 and 4: the ethanol-as-weapon explanation of the Crabtree effect is presented as settled. It is one hypothesis ("make-accumulate-consume"); rate-of-ATP-production and proteome-cost explanations also exist. In question 1, option 2 (faster glucose consumption) is arguably also a correct advantage.
