## Flags

### Suspected factual errors in existing text
1. developmental-signaling-pathways.md, Explainer: "Notch signaling is the only juxtacrine pathway". True only among the four pathways covered; other juxtacrine systems exist (for example Eph/ephrin). Suggest "the only juxtacrine pathway of the four".
2. developmental-timing.md, Q2 (short-answer) answer: "the CDK inhibitor p27 and the transcription factor p57". p57 (Kip2) is a CDK inhibitor, not a transcription factor. The "diluted with each division but accumulate faster than they are diluted" wording is also muddled.
3. epithelial-mesenchymal-transition-developmental.md, Q0 option 1 (the keyed answer): E-cadherin loss "is both necessary and sufficient for the transition". This overstates it; E-cadherin loss alone does not make the full EMT program, and EMT can go ahead with partial E-cadherin retention.
4. epithelial-mesenchymal-transition-developmental.md, Explainer: neural crest "undergo MET to form epithelial structures (like dorsal root ganglia)". Dorsal root ganglia are not usually described as epithelial structures. The example is doubtful.
5. fertilization-and-early-cleavage.md, Explainer: "Polyspermy must be absolutely prevented because it introduces extra chromosomes, which is invariably lethal." Some species are physiologically polyspermic (for example birds and some urodele amphibians), and the extra sperm nuclei degenerate. "Invariably lethal" is too strong.
6. gastrulation.md, Q0 explanation: "Spemann and Mangold's Nobel Prize-winning experiment (1924)". Spemann alone received the 1935 Nobel Prize; Mangold died in 1924. The phrasing implies a shared prize.
7. gastrulation.md, Explainer: "The lateral mesoderm signals to the overlying ectoderm to form skin." Epidermis is generally described as the BMP-driven default of ectoderm without neural induction, not as induced by lateral mesoderm. Doubtful as stated.
8. germ-layer-formation.md, Explainer: "lateral mesoderm becomes kidneys and limb bones". Kidneys come from intermediate mesoderm, not lateral plate mesoderm.
9. organogenesis-basics.md, Q2 explanation: "combining lung epithelium with salivary gland mesenchyme produces lung-type branching but with salivary-gland-type branching pattern, showing that the epithelium determines what organ type forms". This sentence contradicts itself. The classic recombination result is usually summarized as the mesenchyme setting the branching pattern.
10. organogenesis-basics.md, Q0 explanation: "organ-specific identity (lung alveoli vs. kidney nephrons)" from branching epithelium. Nephrons come from the metanephric mesenchyme, not from the branching ureteric epithelium. The example is misleading.
11. pluripotency-and-reprogramming.md, Q2 answer: "Oct4 is the only Yamanaka factor that cannot be replaced by any alternative transcription factor". Later work reported Oct4 substitutes (for example Nr5a2) and Oct4-free reprogramming. The claim is too absolute.
12. regeneration-biology.md, Explainer: "Cut a planarian flatworm into 279 pieces, and each piece regenerates a complete worm." The historical observation is that a fragment about 1/279 of the worm could regenerate. It is not a claim that 279 pieces each regenerate.
13. regeneration-biology.md, Q0 explanation: "Mature muscle fibers ... fragment into mononucleated cells". This is true in newts. In axolotls, limb muscle regenerates mainly from satellite cells, so the claim should not be stated generally for "salamander".
14. stem-cell-biology.md, Explainer: "intestinal stem cells produce the four epithelial cell types of the gut". More types are usually listed (enterocytes, goblet, Paneth, enteroendocrine, tuft, and M cells).
15. induction-and-competence.md, Q2 explanation: loss of competence is "functionally important for preventing teratomas". Unsupported link; consider removing.
16. hox-genes-and-body-plan.md, Explainer: in Drosophila the Hox genes are described as one cluster with labial at the 3' end. The fly Hox genes are split into two complexes (Antennapedia and bithorax). This is a minor simplification.
17. sex-determination.md, Core Idea: Wnt4/RSPO1 is called the "default pathway". Current views treat ovarian development as an active pathway. Minor wording issue.
18. evo-devo.md, Explainer: "feathers evolved from scales". This is contested; feathers and scales are often described as sharing an ancestral skin appendage placode. Minor.

- gene-regulation-prokaryotes -> pluripotency-and-reprogramming (soft): a weak fit. chromatin-in-development or gene-expression-overview would be more relevant.
