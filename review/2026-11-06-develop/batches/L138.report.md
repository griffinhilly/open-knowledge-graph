## Flags

### Suspected factual errors in existing text
1. force-between-parallel-current-wires.md, short-answer Q (index 4) answer and Explainer paragraph 2: "this field points in the −y direction ... F = I₂(ẑ) × (−ŷ) = I₂(x̂) — pointing toward wire 1". For a +z current on the z-axis, the field at +x points along +ŷ, and x̂ points away from a wire at the origin. Correct: B along +ŷ, F ∝ ẑ × ŷ = −x̂, toward wire 1. The two sign errors cancel in the conclusion, but each step is wrong.
2. force-on-current-carrying-conductor.md, Core Idea: "A uniform field exerts net force only on non-planar loops or loops not entirely within the field." Any closed loop in a uniform field has zero net force (∮dl = 0), planar or not. The file's own Explainer and Questions say this.
3. force-on-current-carrying-conductor.md, Q1 (index 1): answer 2 with explanation "Both B and C are essentially correct statements". Two correct options make the item ambiguous.
4. energy-stored-capacitors.md, Core Idea: "Dielectrics are attracted into capacitors because they lower the total stored energy." True only at fixed Q. At fixed V the stored energy rises (the file's own Q1 index 1 and Explainer say so).
5. energy-stored-capacitors.md, Q index 1 explanation and Explainer last paragraph: "forces on dielectrics arise not from direct field forces on bulk material". Physically the pull comes from the fringing field acting on the dielectric's polarization charges. The energy method is a calculation shortcut, not a different mechanism.
6. energy-density-electric-field.md, Q0 explanation: "work is done by the electric field as the plates separate". The field (attraction) does negative work. The energy rises because the external agent does positive work.
7. joule-heating-resistive-power.md, Explainer paragraph 3 and Q index 4 explanation: "longer τ means higher conductivity σ and less Joule heating per unit field". Since p = σE², higher σ gives more heating per unit field (less per unit current density).
8. lorentz-force-on-moving-charge.md, Q index 3 explanation: "The particle moves faster around a tighter circle". Speed is unchanged; it completes orbits more often because the period falls.
9. lenz-law-induced-currents.md, Q index 4 explanation: "a superconducting ring would fall at constant velocity (infinite resistance → perfect Lenz braking) or no braking at all". A superconductor has zero resistance, and the sentence is self-contradictory.
10. faraday-law-electromagnetic-induction.md, Explainer paragraph 2: "the area of the loop can change (as in a generator with a rotating coil)". A rotating coil changes the angle, not the area.
11. gauss-law-problem-solving.md, Explainer paragraph 2: "perpendicular to the flat end-caps, contributing nothing" and "the two flat faces (parallel to E)". The field is parallel to the end-cap surfaces (perpendicular to their normals), and the pillbox faces are perpendicular to E.
12. gauss-law-symmetry.md, Q index 1 explanation: "it ensures E is perpendicular to the end caps (so they contribute zero flux)". Should be parallel to the end-cap surfaces.
13. magnetic-force-lorentz.md, Core Idea: "demonstrating the non-conservative nature of magnetic forces". Circular motion at constant speed does not show non-conservativeness; the magnetic force does no work. Misleading wording.
14. magnetic-dipole-moment.md, Explainer paragraph 3: "the anomalous gyromagnetic ratio of the electron (approximately 2, not 1) was one of the first puzzles that quantum field theory had to explain". The g ≈ 2 value comes from the Dirac equation. The anomaly QFT explains is the small departure from 2 (g − 2).
15. magnetic-torque-dipole.md, Q index 1 explanation: "torque is zero (cos θ appears in τ = μB sin θ ...)". Garbled; the torque uses sin θ.
16. magnetic-field-solenoid.md, Q index 1 explanation and Explainer: "A toroid confines the field exactly to zero outside". True for an ideal toroid. A real helically wound toroid also behaves like one loop around the ring and has a small external field.
17. magnetic-field-lines-flux-density.md, Q index 4 explanation: "you can never 'trap' or 'absorb' magnetic flux". Flux trapping in superconducting rings is a standard effect. Minor.
18. magnetic-force-current-wires.md, Core Idea: "Force per unit length between parallel wires defines ampere." This has been outdated since the 2019 SI redefinition. The file's own Explainer says so.
19. electric-potential-field.md, Explainer paragraph 3: conservativeness is called "a concept from your work on Gauss's law". It comes from the curl-free property, not from Gauss's law. Minor.
