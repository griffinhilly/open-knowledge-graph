## Flags

Suspected factual errors or weak claims in existing text:
- patchwork-girl-materiality-gender.md, Explainer: "The creature in Frankenstein is often read as ambiguous in gender". In Shelley's novel the creature is male. Jackson's protagonist is the female companion Victor starts and destroys. Q2's correct option also calls Frankenstein's monster "a fragmented, female-coded creature", which mixes up the two creatures.
- twine-interactive-platform.md, Explainer: "Twine emerged in the early 2010s". Twine was first released in 2009; its wide uptake came in the early 2010s.
- twine-interactive-platform.md, Explainer and q4 explanation: "set variables visually" / "Variables and logic are implemented visually, not through typed code". In Twine, plain links need only bracket markup, but variables and conditionals are written as typed macros in the story format's markup (for example Harlowe or SugarCube). That is light scripting, not a visual tool.
- twitter-fiction-microfiction.md, Explainer: "This is unprecedented in traditional literature" and q1 explanation "There is no editorial process". Serialized print fiction could already respond to readers between installments. Some Twitter fiction is fully drafted and edited before it is posted on a schedule.
- walking-simulator-form.md, q3 (true-false, index 3): "Walking simulators are 'literary' because they use environmental storytelling and narrative fragments rather than dialogue" is marked true. Many walking simulators rely heavily on voiced narration or dialogue, so the "rather than dialogue" premise is shaky.
- twitter-fiction-platform-constraint-form.md, q1 explanation: "Twitter fiction could not exist on longer-form platforms like blogs" is overstated.
- interactive-fiction-text-adventures.md, Core Idea: "Games like Zork established the form". Colossal Cave Adventure came before Zork. "Like Zork" softens this, but the Explainer says "Zork (1980) established IF as a canonical form". 1980 is the commercial release; the mainframe version dates from the late 1970s.
- Typos in existing YAML (valid, but read oddly): many question strings end with a stray `?"` (for example lipogram-lexical-constraint-writing.md q0 and q1, locative-literature q0 and q1, n-plus-seven q0 and q1, and others in this folder). lipogram-lexical-constraint-writing.md q4 says "how the lipogram function". oulipo-mathematical-constraint.md q4 says "'mathematics enabling rather than diminishing literary meaning.'"
