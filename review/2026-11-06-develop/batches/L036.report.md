## Flags
- snake-lemma-algebraic-topology.md, Core Idea and Explainer: "the long exact sequence of a fibration in homotopy ... all are instances of the snake lemma applied to appropriate short exact sequences of chain complexes." Homotopy groups are not the homology of a chain complex, so the LES of a fibration in homotopy groups does not come from a short exact sequence of chain complexes. (The Serre LES in homology is a different matter.) The claim is overstated.
- snake-lemma-algebraic-topology.md, question 2 (short-answer) explanation: "the rows are the short exact sequences of cycles and boundaries ..., and the vertical maps come from the inclusion of boundaries into cycles." The standard diagram has rows C_n/im ∂ → ... → 0 and 0 → Z_{n-1}(A) → Z_{n-1}(X) → Z_{n-1}(X, A), with vertical maps induced by the boundary ∂. Describing the vertical maps as "inclusion of boundaries into cycles" is wrong. Also "every long exact sequence in homological algebra arises from the snake lemma" is overstated.
- snake-lemma-algebraic-topology.md, Explainer: "Choose a' in A' with a'(a') = g(b_0)" uses the same symbol a' for the map A' → B' and the element. Not a factual error, but confusing.
- universal-coefficient-theorem.md, Explainer paragraph 4: "When G is a field, both Tor and Ext vanish ... H_n(X; k) = H_n(X; Z) tensor k." False for fields of positive characteristic: Tor_1(Z/2, Z/2) = Z/2 and Ext^1(Z/2, Z/2) = Z/2, and the formula without Tor fails at H_2 (H_2(RP^2; Z/2) = Z/2 but H_2(RP^2; Z) ⊗ Z/2 = 0). It is true for G = Q (or any characteristic-0 field). What holds for any field k is H^n(X; k) ≅ Hom_k(H_n(X; k), k), which the Explainer also states. Paragraph 5 repeats the error: "cohomology over a field contains the same information as homology (both Ext and Tor vanish)."
