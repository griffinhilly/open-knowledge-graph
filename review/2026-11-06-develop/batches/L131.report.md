Suspected factual errors in existing text:
- demographic-analysis-and-census-records.md, Explainer: "The U.S. census before 1865 counted enslaved people as three-fifths of a person". The census enumerated enslaved people in full; the three-fifths ratio was applied afterward in apportioning House seats and direct taxes. The sentence conflates the two.
- chronometric-dating-methods.md, Q1 (index 1) explanation: "old-wood effect (the beam came from inner rings of a long-lived tree that died long before the tree was felled)". This garbles the idea. The old-wood effect means the sampled inner rings formed long before the felling date; it is not about the tree dying before it was felled.
- environmental-historical-methods.md, Q1 (index 1) stem: "a sharp increase in tree pollen ... mid-14th century, followed by centuries of lower tree pollen". The "followed by centuries of lower tree pollen" part does not fit the keyed answer (post-plague reforestation) or the Explainer. It reads like a drafting error.
- environmental-proxy-analysis.md, Explainer: "a volcanic eruption causing a 'frost ring' from ash-blocked sunlight". Frost rings are cell damage from freezing during the growing season. Volcanic cooling comes mainly from sulfate aerosols, not ash. The same "volcanic ash blocking sunlight" wording appears in Q0 option B. Also "oxygen isotopes ... varies with evaporation temperature": ice-core d18O is mainly read as a proxy for temperature at condensation and precipitation.
- epigraphic-analysis-and-inscriptions.md, Explainer and Q1/Q3: claims that letter forms alone can date an inscription "to within a generation". This overstates letter-form dating for most material.
- biographical-historical-method.md, Explainer: "the marriages of medieval abbesses" as a prosopography example. Abbesses were professed religious women, so this example is odd. It may mean their family alliances or their origins.
- counter-narrative-analysis.md, Explainer: "slave spiritual songs with coded geographic information". Claims of map-like codes in spirituals (for example "Follow the Drinking Gourd") are disputed by historians. This should be hedged.
- audio-visual-historical-sources.md, Core Idea: "twentieth-century evidence unavailable for earlier periods". Sound recording and film both begin in the late nineteenth century.
- historical-cartography-and-map-analysis.md, Q3 and Explainer generalize Jerusalem-centering to medieval European maps as a whole. This fits the large thirteenth-century mappae mundi (e.g. Hereford), but not every medieval world map.
