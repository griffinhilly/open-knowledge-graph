## Flags

Suspected factual errors in existing text:
- autoethnography.md, question 3 (true-false): `answer: false`, but the explanation begins "This statement is actually true, not false." The statement (autoethnography requires the writer to make their position visible) is true per the Core Idea and Explainer. The answer key should be `true`, and the question prefix 'False:' is misleading.
- capote-narrative-true-crime.md, Core Idea "without falsifying the record", Explainer "He doesn't invent dialogue that wasn't said or attribute motivations without evidence", q2 explanation "The book doesn't invent". Well documented: Capote took no notes or recordings during many interviews and reconstructed dialogue from memory, and critics have shown that some scenes were altered or invented. The best-known case is the closing cemetery scene. The file overstates the book's factual fidelity.
- flash-nonfiction.md, Explainer: "Writers like Robert Olen Butler, Diane Williams, and Dinty W. Moore helped establish flash nonfiction". Butler and Williams are known mainly for flash fiction, not nonfiction. Moore (founding editor of Brevity) fits. The "typically under 1,500 words" figure is also loose; common limits for flash nonfiction are lower (about 750 to 1,000 words), though definitions vary.
- aphoristic-essay-fragments.md, Explainer: the quotation "The only way to deal with an unfree world is to become so absolutely free that your very existence is an act of rebellion" is widely attributed to Camus, but its source is doubtful (it looks like a loose paraphrase). Listing Montaigne among aphorists is also questionable, since his essays are extended and discursive.
- ecocriticism-landscape.md, q3 explanation: it says ecocriticism's "primary aim is to denaturalize nature", not environmental concern. Much ecocriticism has an explicit environmental and ethical commitment. Denaturalizing is one strand, not the field's defining aim.
- biographical-narrative-structure.md, q2 (true-false, answer true): "A biographer who omits unflattering information... is using narrative structure to hide truth". This is too absolute. The item's own explanation says selection is always part of biography, and omission is a problem only when it conceals evidence that matters. Consider rewording the statement or the key.
- didion-writerly-authority.md, Explainer: "Didion has written about..." uses the present perfect for a writer who died in 2021. This is minor.
