## Flags
- domains/history/modern-history/total-war-mobilization-economy.md, Explainer: "Women entered industrial work at scale for the first time". Women had worked in industry in large numbers since early industrialization, notably in textile mills. The WWI change was entry into munitions and heavy industry and into jobs vacated by men, not first entry into industrial work.
- domains/history/modern-history/utopian-socialism.md, Questions item 2 explanation: "In 'The Communist Manifesto' and 'Socialism: Utopian and Scientific,' Marx explicitly contrasted...". Socialism: Utopian and Scientific was written by Engels, not Marx. The question stem's framing of the label as Marx's alone has the same issue; Engels did much to popularize the utopian/scientific contrast.
- domains/history/modern-history/utopian-socialism.md, Core Idea: "They founded experimental communes". Owen did (New Harmony), but Saint-Simon founded none and Fourier's phalansteries were attempted mainly by followers. Overstated as applied to all three.
- domains/history/modern-history/women-equality-rights.md, Explainer and Questions item 0: "In 1960 an American woman could vote, serve on juries". Several states still excluded or exempted women from jury service in 1960 (Hoyt v. Florida, 1961, upheld an automatic exemption).
- domains/history/modern-history/women-equality-rights.md, Explainer: "The Equal Pay Act (1963), Title VII of the Civil Rights Act (1964) ... were direct products of feminist organizing". Both predate most second-wave organizing (NOW was founded in 1966), and the sex provision in Title VII was added by amendment during debate. "Direct products" is overstated for these two.
- domains/history/modern-history/women-equality-rights.md, Explainer: "problem with no name". Friedan's phrase is "the problem that has no name".
- domains/history/modern-history/women-equality-rights.md, Questions item 3 explanation: "The second wave was a deliberate break from, not continuation of, the first wave". Many historians stress continuity (for example, the Equal Rights Amendment campaign ran from the 1920s into the second wave). Stated more absolutely than the scholarship supports.
- domains/history/modern-history/yalta-big-three-settlement.md, Explainer: Soviet armies "occupying Poland, Romania, Bulgaria, and Hungary" in February 1945. Budapest fell on 13 February, after the conference ended, and western Hungary was taken later. "Occupying" Hungary is somewhat overstated for the conference date.
