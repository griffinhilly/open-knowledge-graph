Also Question 0: "a square Pd(II) complex and linear ditopic ligands spontaneously form a [Pd₂L₄]⁴⁺ cage." A cis-protected 90° Pd(II) corner with a linear linker (e.g. 4,4'-bipyridine) gives the [Pd₄L₄] molecular square. [Pd₂L₄] lantern cages come from unprotected Pd(II) with bent (banana-shaped) bis-pyridyl ligands. Also, 4,4'-bipyridine-type linkers are bis-pyridyl, not diamines in the usual sense.
