Stated as universal fact. This is the Caruth-tradition model, contested by Ruth Leys and by memory researchers (e.g. McNally), who find many traumatic memories vivid and narratable. Should be framed as the theory's claim. The same issue affects question 4 (true-false), whose explanation presents the model as settled.
