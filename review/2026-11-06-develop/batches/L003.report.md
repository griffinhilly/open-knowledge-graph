## Flags

### Suspected factual errors in existing text
- renaissance-sonnet-love-form.md, Core Idea: "The Renaissance sonnet tradition, descending from Petrarch, created a 14-line form". The sonnet predates Petrarch (13th-century Sicilian court poets, usually credited to Giacomo da Lentini); Petrarch popularized it rather than creating it.
- renaissance-essay-montaigne.md, Core Idea and T/F item 3: "This form became the dominant vehicle for Enlightenment intellectual expression". Overstated; Enlightenment writers used treatises, encyclopedias, letters, dialogues and novels at least as much as the essay.
- stream-of-consciousness-technique.md, Core Idea ("without grammatical organization") and T/F item 2 ("requires abandoning grammar ... true"). Overstated; much stream-of-consciousness writing (e.g. Woolf) stays grammatical and uses free indirect discourse. Only some passages (e.g. the final episode of Ulysses) drop punctuation and syntax. The true-false answer is arguably wrong.
- romanticism-movement-overview.md, MC item 0: "Industrial mechanization in manufacturing" is treated as a wrong distractor. Romanticism is widely read as partly a reaction against industrialization, so the item is ambiguous.
- troubadour-courtly-love-occitan.md, Core Idea/Explainer: "elevated spiritual longing ... above carnal satisfaction" and "Possession or physical satisfaction would diminish the love". Contested; many troubadour songs openly desire physical union, and some early troubadour verse is bawdy. "All subsequent European lyric poetry" / "Every subsequent love poet" is also overstated.
- troubadour-occitan-tradition.md, Explainer: "something unprecedented in medieval European culture: a sophisticated ... tradition composed entirely in a vernacular". Old English and Old Irish poetry are earlier sophisticated vernacular traditions.
- victorian-poetry-anxiety-doubt.md, Explainer: "new possibilities for poetry in a post-Christian age" and "For many educated Victorians, faith became impossible". Overstated; Victorian Britain remained largely Christian, and major poets (Hopkins, Christina Rossetti) were devout.
- renaissance-humanist-recovery-classical.md, Explainer: "Dante had done this with classical epic form". Dante is usually classed as late medieval, and the Commedia is not in classical epic form (terza rima, Christian allegory). The Explainer's "shifted cultural emphasis from religious salvation to secular accomplishment" also sits uneasily with its own T/F item 3.
- postmodernism-metafiction-self-reflexivity.md, Explainer: "what realism and modernism tried to hide: that representation is always constructed". Modernism was itself highly self-conscious about form, so this claim is doubtful for modernism.
- realist-novel-objective-representation.md, Core Idea: realist novelists "developed" free indirect discourse. The technique was already used extensively before mid-century realism (notably by Jane Austen); realists extended it.
