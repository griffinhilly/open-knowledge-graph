## E. THE VALIDATION HARNESS (Synth 3's core contribution — required before any derived profile reaches a user under Option A/C)

The architecture buys REPRODUCIBILITY, not VALIDITY: a wrong seed prior produces thousands of smooth, plausible, *consistently-wrong, uncancelled* profiles. Three **pre-registered** gates (write pass criteria BEFORE seeing output — the same apophenia that sinks C5 sinks a post-hoc validation):

- **G1 — Face-validity hold-out (necessary, weak).** 20-30 topics with uncontroversial dominant faculty ("mental rotation"→spatial, "writing a proof"→axiomatic). Derived profile must rank the right faculty on top. Pre-register anchors + threshold.
- **G2 — Convergent validity via promoted B7 (the key upgrade).** Factorize the engine's 15K-profile matrix per stage (NMF/sparse-PCA); ask whether the graph's OWN topology independently recovers something close to the hand-authored A3 basis. Recovery = genuine evidence the structure is real, not imposed (literally Griffin's "discovery over invention" ethic). Orthogonal = falsification signal. Pre-register the alignment criterion (e.g., cosine sim between factors and seed columns) — NMF bases are rotation-ambiguous, so eyeballing = factor-level apophenia.
- **G3 — Faculty ⊥ Domain discriminant test (decides whether the consumer claim is even MEANINGFUL).** Do same-domain/same-stage topics show DIFFERENT faculty profiles (good — faculty adds information beyond the domain label) or does profile collapse to the domain label (fatal — "this needs conservation" carries no info beyond "this is chemistry," and the whole value prop is hollow)?
- **Plus correctness precondition:** the magnitude/altitude channel (shape AND size).
- **Hard corollary:** consumer surfaces do not proceed unless G1∧G2∧G3 pass. Failure is the truth-test working, not a project failure.
