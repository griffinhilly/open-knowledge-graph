# Spontaneity and ΔG

## Core Idea
The Gibbs free energy change (ΔG = ΔH - TΔS) combines enthalpy and entropy to predict spontaneity. A negative ΔG indicates a spontaneous process; positive ΔG indicates non-spontaneous.
