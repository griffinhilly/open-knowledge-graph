# Narrow Content and Mental Individuation

## Core Idea
Narrow content theory proposes that what a mental state represents depends only on the individual's intrinsic, internal physical properties—not on the external environment. On this view, your thoughts about water would be identical to your thoughts about 'twater' if your intrinsic brain states were identical, even though water and twater are different.

## How It's Best Learned
Examine Twin Earth scenarios carefully. Consider what role the body, brain, and environment play in determining thought content.

## Common Misconceptions
- Assuming narrow content is obviously true.
- Thinking narrow content requires representationalism.
- Confusing narrow content with introspective content.
