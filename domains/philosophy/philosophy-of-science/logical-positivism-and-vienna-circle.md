# Logical Positivism and the Vienna Circle

## Core Idea
Logical positivists attempted to place science on secure philosophical foundations by combining empiricism with formal logic. They argued that meaningful scientific statements must be either analytically true (true by definition) or empirically verifiable through observation. This movement dominated Anglo-American philosophy in the early 20th century and shaped science policy and education.
