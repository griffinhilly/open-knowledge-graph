# Philosophy of Neuroscience

## Core Idea
Neuroscience promises to explain consciousness, thought, and behavior through brain mechanisms. Philosophy of neuroscience examines whether such explanations are possible and what they reveal about mind. Does neural mapping constitute genuine explanation, or does it commit the mereological fallacy? How do neural mechanisms relate to subjective experiences (the hard problem of consciousness)? Does neuroscientific reductionism undermine notions of agency, responsibility, and the self?
