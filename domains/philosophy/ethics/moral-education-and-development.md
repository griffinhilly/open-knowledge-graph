# Moral Education and Development

## Core Idea
How do moral virtues develop? Not primarily through instruction but through habituation, mentorship, reflective practice, and emotional cultivation. A person becomes virtuous by repeatedly acting virtuously until virtuous response becomes second nature. This developmental view shapes how we think about moral education, parenting, and social institutions. It emphasizes practice and modeling over mere knowledge, connecting ethics to human development.

## How It's Best Learned
Contrast learning virtue with learning facts: you don't read about courage in a book and become courageous. What does it take to cultivate character? How do role models facilitate development?

## Common Misconceptions
Treating habituation as mere repetition without understanding. Assuming moral development ends in childhood. Overlooking the role of reflection in virtue acquisition.
