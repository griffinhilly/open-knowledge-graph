# Sources of Moral Disagreement

## Core Idea
Why do moral agents persistently disagree despite good faith reasoning? Sources include empirical disagreements about facts, different foundational frameworks or principles, cultural backgrounds that shape moral perspective, cognitive biases and motivated reasoning, and (if realism is true) reasonable epistemic limits on moral knowledge. Understanding sources helps evaluate whether disagreement supports moral relativism, reveals rational limits on moral certainty, or indicates merely resolvable empirical confusion.
