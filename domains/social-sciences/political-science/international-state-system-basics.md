# The International State System and Anarchic Order

## Core Idea
The international state system is an anarchic order of sovereign states with no overarching authority, requiring states to ensure their own security and interests through diplomacy, alliances, and military power. The Westphalian system created the principle of sovereign equality, though this ideal is modified by power asymmetries and international institutions.
