# Core Principles of Democratic Governance

## Core Idea
Democracy is a system where political power ultimately rests with the people. Core principles include popular sovereignty, political equality, responsive institutions, and mechanisms for collective decision-making. Democratic systems differ in how directly or representatively they realize these principles.

## How It's Best Learned
Compare ancient democratic practices (Athenian direct democracy) with modern representative democracy. Study democratic breakdowns and understand what principles are violated. Examine hybrid systems claiming democracy while limiting some principles.
