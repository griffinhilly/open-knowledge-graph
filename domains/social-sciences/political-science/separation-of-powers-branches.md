# Separation of Powers and Checks and Balances

## Core Idea
The separation of powers divides government authority into three branches—executive (enforcement), legislative (lawmaking), and judicial (interpretation)—each with distinct functions and some ability to check the others. This institutional design aims to prevent the concentration of power and protect individual liberties by requiring multiple institutions to agree on major decisions.
