# Representation and Democratic Linkage

## Core Idea
Representation is the relationship between citizens and elected officials; representatives may adopt a delegate role (voting constituents' preferences), a trustee role (exercising independent judgment in constituent interest), or a partisan role (following party leadership). Democratic linkage—the mechanisms connecting citizen preferences to policy—operates through elections, political parties, and interest groups. Democracies vary in how directly preferences translate to outcomes: some systems prioritize mandates from elections, others emphasize deliberative judgment by representatives. The concept of representation itself is contested between those seeing it as mechanism for expressing constituent preferences and those seeing it as locus of deliberation and judgment.
