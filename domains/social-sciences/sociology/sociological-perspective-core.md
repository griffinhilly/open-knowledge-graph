# The Sociological Perspective: Core Principles

## Core Idea
The sociological perspective views human behavior as shaped by social forces and structures rather than individual choice alone. It emphasizes that what appears as personal troubles often reflects broader social patterns, and understanding society requires examining how institutions, groups, and cultural norms influence individual action.
