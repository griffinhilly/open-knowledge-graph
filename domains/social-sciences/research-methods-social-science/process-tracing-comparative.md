# Process Tracing in Comparative Research

## Core Idea
Process tracing reconstructs the causal chain linking independent variables to outcomes through qualitative investigation of within-case evidence. Rather than comparing cases to infer causation, process tracing examines documentary evidence, interviews, and archival records to confirm or disconfirm hypothesized mechanisms. Bayesian process tracing uses Bayes' theorem to assess how evidence updates beliefs about competing causal hypotheses. Process tracing excels at identifying necessary and sufficient conditions.
