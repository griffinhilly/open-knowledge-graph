# Grounded Theory: Constant Comparison and Coding

## Core Idea
Grounded theory develops theories from qualitative data through iterative coding and comparison. Constant comparison—identifying similarities and differences across instances—builds increasingly abstract conceptualizations. Theory emerges from data rather than being imposed beforehand.
