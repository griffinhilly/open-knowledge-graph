# Bias, Positionality, and Reflexivity: Researcher Influence

## Core Idea
Researchers are not neutral observers. Social positions, identities, and assumptions influence research questions, data collection, and interpretation. Reflexivity—systematic reflection on these influences—is a methodological resource. Acknowledging positionality enhances credibility and validity.
