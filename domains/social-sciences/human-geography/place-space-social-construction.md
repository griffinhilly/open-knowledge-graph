# Place, Space, and Social Construction

## Core Idea
Place differs from space: space is abstract location while place is space invested with meaning, emotion, and social significance. Places are socially constructed through human activities, memory, and representation rather than naturally given. Understanding how people attach meaning to locations reveals how geography shapes social life.
