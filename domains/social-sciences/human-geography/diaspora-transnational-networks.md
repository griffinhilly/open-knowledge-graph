# Diaspora and Transnational Communities

## Core Idea
Diaspora communities—populations dispersed from homelands—remain connected across space through networks of kinship, business, culture, and politics. These transnational networks link multiple places and challenge the assumption that people belong to single nation-states. Diasporas create cultural hybridity, economic flows of remittances, and political movements that cross borders.
