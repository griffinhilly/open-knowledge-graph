# Colonialism and Geographic Patterns of Inequality

## Core Idea
Colonialism created geographic patterns of inequality that persist in postcolonial states. Colonial powers extracted resources, established boundaries, and imposed economic systems that advantaged colonizers. Postcolonial geographies inherit these inequalities, visible in borders, cities, economies, and social hierarchies.
