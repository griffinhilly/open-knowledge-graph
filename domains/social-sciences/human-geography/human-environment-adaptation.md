# Human-Environment Adaptation and Systems

## Core Idea
Humans adapt to diverse environmental conditions through cultural practices, technology, and social organization, but environments also constrain and shape human possibilities. Adaptation is bidirectional: humans modify environments while adapting to them. Understanding coupled human-environment systems requires integrating biophysical and social analysis.
