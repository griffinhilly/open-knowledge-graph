# Population Distribution and Geographic Processes

## Core Idea
Human populations are unevenly distributed across Earth, with concentrations in specific regions reflecting historical, economic, and environmental factors. Population growth rates, migration patterns, and age structures vary dramatically by region. Understanding these patterns requires examining both physical geography and social-historical processes.
