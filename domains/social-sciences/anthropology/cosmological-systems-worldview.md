# Cosmological Systems and Worldviews

## Core Idea
A cosmology is a culture's comprehensive system of understanding the nature of reality, the universe, deities or spirits, and humanity's place within it. Studying cosmologies reveals how different cultures organize fundamental categories of being, time, space, causality, and morality, demonstrating that scientific worldviews are themselves culturally particular cosmologies.
