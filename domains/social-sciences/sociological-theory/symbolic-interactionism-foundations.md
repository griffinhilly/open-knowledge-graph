# Symbolic Interactionism: Foundations

## Core Idea
Symbolic interactionism posits that humans act based on meanings they assign to things, and these meanings arise from social interaction. People continuously interpret and re-interpret the world, creating and modifying meanings through social processes. Society is built through countless interactions using symbols like language and gestures.
