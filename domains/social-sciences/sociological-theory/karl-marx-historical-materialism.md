# Karl Marx and Historical Materialism

## Core Idea
Marx's historical materialism posits that economic systems and the material conditions of production are the primary drivers of social change and cultural formations. Societies progress through stages (primitive communism, slavery, feudalism, capitalism, socialism) determined by changes in how goods are produced and distributed.
