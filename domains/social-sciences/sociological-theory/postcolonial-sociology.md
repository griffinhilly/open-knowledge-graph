# Postcolonial Sociology

## Core Idea
Postcolonial sociology examines how colonialism's legacies shape contemporary global inequalities, institutions, and knowledge production. It critiques sociology as a Western-centric discipline that universalized particular (European) ways of knowing. Postcolonial scholars center non-Western perspectives and explore how colonized peoples resisted and created alternative modernities.

## How It's Best Learned
Examine a contemporary institution or practice with colonial history: How do colonial structures persist? What indigenous or alternative forms exist?

## Common Misconceptions
Postcolonial theory isn't just historical—it analyzes ongoing neocolonial relationships and contemporary knowledge hierarchies.
