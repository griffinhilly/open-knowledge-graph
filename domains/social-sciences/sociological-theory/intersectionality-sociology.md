# Intersectionality in Sociological Analysis

## Core Idea
Intersectionality examines how multiple systems of oppression (gender, race, class, sexuality, disability) interact in ways that can't be reduced to sum of individual oppressions. An individual's position results from simultaneous location in multiple hierarchies. Different intersectional positions produce distinct experiences and constraints even within the same demographic category.

## How It's Best Learned
Analyze a social phenomenon (poverty, education, incarceration) examining how it's shaped by intersecting systems. How does outcome vary by intersectional position?

## Common Misconceptions
Intersectionality isn't just adding up identities—it's analyzing how systems of domination mutually constitute each other.
