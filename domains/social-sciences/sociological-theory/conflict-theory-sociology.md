# Conflict Theory in Sociology

## Core Idea
Conflict theory views society as arenas of competition over scarce resources and power. Rather than assuming shared values maintain order, it sees coercion and structural inequality as foundational. Institutions serve dominant groups; change emerges through struggles between classes, races, genders, and other groups with opposed interests.

## How It's Best Learned
Analyze an institution through conflict perspective: who benefits? Who is harmed? What power dynamics sustain it?

## Common Misconceptions
Conflict theory isn't saying all interaction is hostile—it's identifying systematic inequalities and structural antagonisms beneath apparent consensus.
