# Frankfurt School and Critical Theory

## Core Idea
The Frankfurt School (Horkheimer, Adorno, Marcuse) analyzed how capitalism reproduces domination through culture and ideology rather than just coercion. Mass media and mass culture create false consciousness and integrate workers into capitalist system. Critical theory exposes how what appears natural or inevitable actually serves particular interests.

## How It's Best Learned
Analyze a cultural product (advertisement, film, news) as ideology. What interests does it serve? What does it naturalize?

## Common Misconceptions
Critical theory isn't claiming ordinary people are dupes—it's examining structural mechanisms through which domination operates.
