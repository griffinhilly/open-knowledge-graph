# Alienation in Marxist Theory

## Core Idea
Alienation describes how capitalism separates workers from the products of their labor, from the labor process itself, from their species-being, and from each other. Workers become commodities, their creativity is stolen, and labor becomes instrumental suffering rather than self-expression.

## How It's Best Learned
Examine concrete cases: factory workers, gig economy, knowledge workers. How does alienation manifest across different labor contexts?

## Common Misconceptions
Alienation isn't just feeling disconnected—it's structural estrangement baked into capitalist production relations.
