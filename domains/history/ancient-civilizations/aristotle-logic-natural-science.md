# Aristotle, Logic, and Natural Philosophy

## Core Idea
Aristotle (384-322 BCE) developed formal logic (the syllogism) as a systematic method for reasoning and established natural philosophy—the systematic study of nature through observation and classification. His work created frameworks for organizing knowledge that dominated Western thought for nearly two millennia.

## How It's Best Learned
Study Aristotle's categorical system and syllogistic logic to understand his reasoning methods. Examine his classification of animals and plants to see how he applied observation to organize knowledge.

## Common Misconceptions
Aristotle did not rely purely on observation—his system of logic and his conclusions about physics were often based on theoretical reasoning, and many were later proven incorrect.
