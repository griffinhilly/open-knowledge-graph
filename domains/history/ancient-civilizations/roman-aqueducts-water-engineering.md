# Roman Aqueducts and Water Engineering

## Core Idea
Roman aqueducts transported water from distant sources to cities through gravity-fed channels, arches, and pipes, supplying public baths, fountains, and homes. This engineering achievement required mathematical precision, organized labor, and capital investment, demonstrating Roman technological capability and organizational power.

## How It's Best Learned
Study specific aqueducts (Pont du Gard, Aqua Claudia) through archaeological evidence and engineering analysis. Calculate gradients and water volume to understand the technical sophistication.

## Common Misconceptions
Roman aqueducts were not the first water systems, but they were notably sophisticated and widely distributed throughout the empire. Many were built over centuries with continuous refinement.
