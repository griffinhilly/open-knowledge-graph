# Roman Legal Codes and the Justice System

## Core Idea
Roman law evolved from the Twelve Tables (c. 450 BCE) through extensive codification, establishing concepts like written law, property rights, contracts, and procedures for legal appeals. Roman legal principles—codified most comprehensively in Justinian's Code (6th century CE)—fundamentally shaped European and modern legal systems.

## How It's Best Learned
Study specific legal principles and cases from Roman sources to understand how the system actually functioned. Trace the evolution from early codes to later compilations.

## Common Misconceptions
Roman law, while sophisticated, was not universally applied—it favored property owners and citizens while excluding slaves and non-citizens from most protections.
