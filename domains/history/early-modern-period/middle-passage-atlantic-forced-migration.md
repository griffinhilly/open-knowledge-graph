# The Middle Passage: Atlantic Slave Trade and Forced Migration

## Core Idea
The Middle Passage—the horrific transatlantic voyage transporting enslaved Africans to the Americas—killed millions through disease, malnutrition, and violence while creating diaspora communities whose labor built colonial wealth. The Middle Passage exemplifies the brutal mechanics of early-modern racial slavery and Atlantic commerce.
