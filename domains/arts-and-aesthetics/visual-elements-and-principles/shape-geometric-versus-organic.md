# Shape: Geometric and Organic

## Core Idea
Shapes are enclosed areas with recognizable edges and form. Geometric shapes (circle, square, triangle, polygon) have regular, precise edges and suggest order, stability, and artificiality. Organic shapes are irregular and curved, inspired by living things and nature, suggesting growth, movement, and naturalness. The choice between geometric and organic shapes communicates meaning.

## How It's Best Learned
Collect images showing both geometric and organic shapes from design, nature, and art. Trace and redraw both types to understand their characteristics. Create compositions using primarily one type, then mix them and observe how the feeling changes.

## Common Misconceptions
Geometric shapes are more skillful to draw or more appropriate for formal work. Organic shapes must look perfectly natural; slightly imperfect shapes don't read as organic. Shapes must be mathematically perfect to be geometric.
