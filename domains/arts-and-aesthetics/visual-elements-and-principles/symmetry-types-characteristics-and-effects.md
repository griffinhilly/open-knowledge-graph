# Symmetry Types and Their Compositional Effects

## Core Idea
Symmetry exists in multiple forms—bilateral (mirror), radial (rotational), and translational—each creating different visual effects. Bilateral symmetry feels formal and balanced. Radial symmetry feels dynamic and centered. Approximate or broken symmetry creates tension and interest. Understanding symmetry types allows artists to control the emotional tone and visual impact of their compositions.

## How It's Best Learned
Create compositions using different symmetry types and analyze how each feels different emotionally and visually.

## Common Misconceptions
Equating symmetry with perfection; not recognizing that broken symmetry can be more interesting than perfect symmetry.
