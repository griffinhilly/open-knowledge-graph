# Installation Art

## Core Idea
Installation art transforms an entire space into an immersive, site-specific artwork. Rather than viewing discrete objects, viewers enter an environment that has been intentionally designed to create a particular experience.

## Explainer

Installation art creates immersive environments that transform a space. Rather than creating discrete objects, installation artists redesign the entire room or landscape. Viewers enter the artwork and experience it with their bodies. Key characteristics include site-specificity, immersion, phenomenological engagement, and often ephemerality. Artists manipulate light and perception, allowing viewers to move through transformed spaces. The artwork is the lived experience of entering and moving through the environment.
