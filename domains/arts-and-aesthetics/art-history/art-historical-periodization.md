# Periodization in Art History

## Core Idea
Art history is organized into periods (Ancient, Medieval, Renaissance, Baroque, Modern, Contemporary) defined by shared stylistic, social, and cultural characteristics. These divisions help us understand patterns of change and continuity, but they are analytical frameworks rather than absolute boundaries—different cultures and media may follow different periodization schemes. Understanding periodization means recognizing both its utility for analysis and its limitations as a historical tool.
