# Still Life: History and Meaning

## Core Idea
Still life emerged as a distinct genre in 16th-century Europe, initially dismissed as a 'lower' genre before gaining prestige in 17th-century Dutch and Spanish painting. Still life compositions are never merely neutral representations—they encode meanings through symbolic associations (wilting flowers suggesting mortality) and display cultural values (exotic goods displaying wealth). The still life tradition reveals what cultures valued, preserved, and celebrated in visual form.
