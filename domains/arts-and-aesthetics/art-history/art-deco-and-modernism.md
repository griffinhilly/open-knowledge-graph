# Art Deco: Geometric Modernism and Machine Aesthetics

## Core Idea
Art Deco (1920s-1930s) synthesized geometric abstraction, machine-age aesthetics, and streamlined forms in architecture, graphics, and industrial design. Where Art Nouveau embraced organic curves, Art Deco celebrated crystalline geometry and symmetrical composition as expressions of modernity, progress, and industrial rationality.
