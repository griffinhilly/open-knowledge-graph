# Historical and Social Context in Art

## Core Idea
Artworks emerge from specific historical moments shaped by economics, politics, religious beliefs, technology, and social structures. Contextualizing art means understanding not only what is depicted but why it was made, who made it, who commissioned it, and how people of its time experienced it. This contextual reading often reveals meanings and intentions invisible when viewing art outside its original setting.
