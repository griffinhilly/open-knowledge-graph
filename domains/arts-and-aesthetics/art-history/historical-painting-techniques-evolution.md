# Evolution of Painting Techniques Across Historical Periods

## Core Idea
Painting techniques evolved as artists discovered new materials (oils, synthetic pigments), developed methods (underpainting, glazing, impasto), and responded to aesthetic goals. Medieval fresco required different approaches than Renaissance tempera; Impressionism emerged partly from portable paints and faster-drying oils. Understanding technical innovation reveals artistic constraints and possibilities in different periods, and shows how material culture shapes aesthetic achievement.
