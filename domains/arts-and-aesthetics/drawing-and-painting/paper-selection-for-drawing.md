# Paper Selection for Drawing

## Core Idea
Paper varies in weight, texture, and composition, each affecting how drawing media behaves. Heavyweight papers (140 lb+) resist tearing and buckling; rough surfaces grip graphite, while smooth surfaces allow fine detail. Acid-free papers ensure longevity. Selecting appropriate paper for the medium—smooth for pen, textured for charcoal—enhances the final work and prevents frustration.

## How It's Best Learned
Purchase sample packs of drawing papers and test them with different media: pencil, charcoal, ink, and markers.

## Common Misconceptions
Believing any paper will work for any medium. Charcoal on smooth paper slides; pen on rough paper splays. Match the paper tooth to the medium.
