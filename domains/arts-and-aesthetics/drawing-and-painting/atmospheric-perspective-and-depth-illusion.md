# Atmospheric Perspective and Depth Illusion

## Core Idea
Atmospheric perspective mimics how air and distance affect appearance: distant objects become lighter, less detailed, and more blue-gray; nearby objects are darker, sharper, and more saturated. By layering these cues with linear perspective, you create convincing landscape and interior depth, especially in painting.

## How It's Best Learned
Observe actual distance in landscapes. Practice painting distant mountains lighter and bluer, near trees darker and warmer.
