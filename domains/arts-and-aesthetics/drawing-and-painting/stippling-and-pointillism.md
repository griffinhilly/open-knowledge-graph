# Stippling and Pointillism

## Core Idea
Stippling is a technique that builds tone and form using individual dots or small marks rather than lines. The density and spacing of dots create perceived value, allowing artists to render detailed forms with patience and precision. It requires understanding how the eye optically blends separated marks.

## How It's Best Learned
Begin with light subjects to avoid hours of repetitive work. Use a consistent dot size initially, then experiment with varying mark size to create depth.

## Common Misconceptions
Thinking all dots must be identical in size and spacing. Larger or varied dots often create more interesting and realistic effects.
