# Figure Foreshortening in Practice

## Core Idea
Body parts pointing toward or away from the viewer appear compressed and shortened. Understanding foreshortening allows drawing convincing figures from extreme angles. Perspective principles apply directly to the human form: receding parts shrink; overlapping clarifies depth.
