# Visual Semiotics in Design

## Core Idea
Visual semiotics examines how design creates meaning through signs—the relationship between a visual form, what it represents, and the meaning users derive from it. Every icon, color choice, and graphical element carries cultural and contextual meaning that shapes user interpretation. Understanding these sign systems allows designers to encode intended meaning and anticipate user understanding.

## How It's Best Learned
Study icons and logos across cultures to see how the same form means different things in different contexts. Analyze warning signs and symbols to understand how successful designs make meaning explicit.

## Common Misconceptions
That visual meaning is universal. In fact, meaning depends heavily on cultural context, previous experience, and familiarity.
