# Emotional Resonance Through Design

## Core Idea
Design choices—color palettes, typography, imagery, and spacing—evoke emotional responses in viewers. Warm colors and curved lines feel approachable; cool colors and sharp angles feel formal or edgy. Awareness of these associations allows designers to align emotional tone with intent.
