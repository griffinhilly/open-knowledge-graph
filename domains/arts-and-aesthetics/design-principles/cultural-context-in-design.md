# Cultural Context in Design

## Core Idea
Design always exists within cultural contexts that shape how users interpret symbols, colors, hierarchies, and interactions. Colors carry different connotations across cultures; reading direction affects layout; interaction patterns reflect cultural values. Effective design acknowledges and respects these contexts rather than assuming one global standard. Ignoring cultural context leads to misinterpretation and failure.

## How It's Best Learned
Research how the same brand adapts visual design across different cultural markets. Document how colors, imagery, and patterns differ and why.

## Common Misconceptions
That 'international design' means using neutral, sterile aesthetics. Actually, culturally aware design can be more distinctive and resonant.
