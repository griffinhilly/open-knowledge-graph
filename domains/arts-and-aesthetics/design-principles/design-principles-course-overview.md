# Design Principles Course Overview

## Core Idea
Design principles are the fundamental rules and guidelines that govern how visual elements are organized and arranged to create effective communication. These seven core principles—balance, contrast, emphasis, movement, pattern, proportion, and unity—are universal tools that apply across all visual design disciplines. Understanding these principles provides the conceptual framework for making intentional design decisions rather than arranging elements arbitrarily.

## How It's Best Learned
Examine designs in the wild—posters, websites, magazine layouts, product packaging—and identify which principles are at work. Notice how removing or emphasizing a principle changes the effectiveness of the design.

## Common Misconceptions
- Design principles are rules to never break; in reality, they are tools to use strategically.
- Principles are only relevant to fine art; they apply equally to functional, commercial, and digital design.
- Learning principles requires memorizing definitions; it requires recognizing them in context.
