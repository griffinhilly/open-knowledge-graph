# Minimalism and Reduction in Design

## Core Idea
Minimalism in design is the practice of removing all non-essential elements while retaining functional and aesthetic integrity—often summarized as 'less is more.' This principle reduces cognitive load, improves clarity, and often produces elegant solutions. Minimalism requires strong judgment about what is truly essential versus what is merely habitual or decorative.

## How It's Best Learned
Create two versions of a design—one with all details, one stripped to essentials. Observe which communicates more clearly and note what you actually missed removing.

## Common Misconceptions
That minimalist design is always black-and-white or that it lacks personality. Minimalism can be warm, colorful, and distinctive.
