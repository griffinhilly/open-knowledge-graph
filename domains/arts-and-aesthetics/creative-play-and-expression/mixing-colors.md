# Mixing Colors

## Core Idea
When you mix two colors together, you get a brand new color! Red and yellow make orange. Blue and yellow make green. Red and blue make purple. Mixing colors is like magic, and it helps you make even more colors for your art.

## How It's Best Learned
Let children mix paint colors on a palette or paper plate. Start with red, yellow, and blue paint. Ask "What do you think will happen if we mix these two?" Let them experiment and discover. Use finger paint or washable tempera for easy mixing and cleanup.

## Common Misconceptions
- Mixing all colors together makes a rainbow; mixing many colors together usually makes brown or gray.
- You can unmix colors back to what they were; once colors are mixed, they stay mixed.
- There is only one way to make green; you can make many different greens by using more or less yellow and blue.

## Explainer

**Mixing colors** is one of the most exciting things you can do with paint. You start with two colors, swirl them together, and a brand new color appears! It feels like magic, but it is really science and art working together.

**There are three very special colors called primary colors: red, yellow, and blue.** You cannot make these colors by mixing other colors. But you can mix them together to make new colors! Red and yellow make **orange**. Blue and yellow make **green**. Red and blue make **purple**. These new colors are called **secondary colors**.

**You can make even more colors by changing how much of each you use.** A little bit of blue mixed with a lot of yellow makes a bright, light green. A lot of blue mixed with a little yellow makes a deep, dark green. You are in charge! You can create your own special colors by experimenting with how much of each color you add.

**Try mixing colors with paint, playdough, or even colored water.** Squeeze two colors of playdough together and knead them. Drop food coloring into cups of water. Swirl finger paints on paper. Every time you mix, you learn something new. And remember, if you mix too many colors together, you will probably get brown, which is a perfectly good color too!
