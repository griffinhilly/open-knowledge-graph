# Formalism in Aesthetics

## Core Idea
Formalism (Bell, Fry, Greenberg) argues that aesthetic value lies in pure formal properties—line, color, shape, composition, spatial relations—independent of representation, narrative, or external content. This approach positions visual form as intrinsically meaningful and elevates abstract and modernist art. It insists that aesthetic experience is sui generis and cannot be reduced to intellectual or emotional content.
