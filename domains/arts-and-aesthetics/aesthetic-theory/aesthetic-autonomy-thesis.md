# Aesthetic Autonomy Thesis

## Core Idea
Modernist aesthetic theory claims that artworks are self-sufficient objects whose meaning is independent of external social, political, or moral contexts. This autonomy thesis, central to formalism and modernism, posits that aesthetic value derives solely from intrinsic formal properties rather than historical circumstances or artistic intention.

## How It's Best Learned
Study Greenberg's criticism alongside more recent critiques that challenge autonomy; examine how the artist's biography or political moment affects interpretation.

## Common Misconceptions
Aesthetic autonomy does not mean art is apolitical; the claim itself is a historical position with ideological functions, not a timeless truth about art.
