# Relational Aesthetics

## Core Idea
Relational aesthetics (Bourriaud) defines art as social encounters, participatory events, and intersubjective experiences rather than discrete aesthetic objects. Artworks function as catalysts for human connection, temporary communities, and utopian social spaces. Aesthetic value shifts from formal innovation to the quality and intensity of relational encounters generated by the work.
