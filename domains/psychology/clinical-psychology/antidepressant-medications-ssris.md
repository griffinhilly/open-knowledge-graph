# Antidepressant Medications: SSRIs and Mechanisms

## Core Idea
Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors (SSRIs) are first-line antidepressants that block serotonin reuptake, increasing synaptic serotonin. SSRIs are effective for depression, anxiety, and OCD with generally tolerable side effects. Understanding SSRI mechanisms, side effects, and individual variation in response is essential for clinical practice.
