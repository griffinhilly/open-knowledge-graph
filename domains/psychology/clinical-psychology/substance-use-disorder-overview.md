# Substance Use Disorders: Overview and Classification

## Core Idea
Substance Use Disorders involve a maladaptive pattern of substance use leading to clinically significant impairment, characterized by tolerance, withdrawal, loss of control, and continued use despite negative consequences. SUDs vary by substance class (alcohol, opioids, stimulants, hallucinogens, cannabis) and severity. The biopsychosocial model integrates genetic vulnerability, learned associations with drug cues, and environmental contingencies.
