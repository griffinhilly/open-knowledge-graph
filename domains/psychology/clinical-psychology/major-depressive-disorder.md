# Major Depressive Disorder

## Core Idea
Major Depressive Disorder involves depressed mood and/or anhedonia with neurovegetative and cognitive symptoms causing significant functional impairment. It reflects dysfunction in reward processing, negative cognitive biases, and monoamine dysregulation.
