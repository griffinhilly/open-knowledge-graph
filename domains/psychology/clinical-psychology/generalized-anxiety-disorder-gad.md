# Generalized Anxiety Disorder

## Core Idea
Generalized Anxiety Disorder involves persistent, uncontrollable worry about multiple domains lasting at least 6 months, accompanied by physical symptoms. GAD is maintained by worry cycles that prevent habituation. Understanding cognitive and physiological aspects is essential for assessment and intervention.
