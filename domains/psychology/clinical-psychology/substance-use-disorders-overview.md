# Substance Use Disorders: Overview and Neurobiology

## Core Idea
Substance Use Disorders involve problematic use patterns leading to clinical impairment and compulsive use despite consequences. Modern understanding emphasizes dopamine dysregulation, prefrontal cortex dysfunction, and the transition from recreational to compulsive use.
