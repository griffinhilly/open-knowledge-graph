# Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

## Core Idea
PTSD develops after exposure to a traumatic event and involves re-experiencing (flashbacks, nightmares), avoidance of reminders, negative mood/cognition changes, and hyperarousal. The amygdala-mediated fear response becomes conditioned to trauma cues, while the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex fail to properly contextualize and extinction-learn the memory. Complex trauma from repeated interpersonal violence presents with additional features affecting identity and relationships.
