# Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

## Core Idea
OCD involves intrusive, unwanted thoughts (obsessions) triggering anxiety and repetitive mental or behavioral responses (compulsions). Modern formulations emphasize dysfunctional beliefs and how compulsions paradoxically strengthen the OCD cycle.
