# Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Theory and Application

## Core Idea
CBT is based on the principle that thoughts, feelings, and behaviors are interconnected; changing maladaptive thoughts and behaviors improves emotional functioning. It involves structured assessment, behavioral experiments, and cognitive restructuring.
