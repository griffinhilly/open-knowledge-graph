# Clinical Assessment and Diagnosis

## Core Idea
Clinical assessment integrates information from interviews, tests, observations, and collateral sources to understand a client's presenting problems, history, and functioning. Reliable assessment requires understanding measurement principles and clinician bias; assessment guides diagnosis but is not diagnosis itself. Validity depends on instrument selection, interpretation accuracy, and integration across methods.
