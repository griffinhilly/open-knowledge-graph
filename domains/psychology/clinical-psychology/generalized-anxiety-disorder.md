# Generalized Anxiety Disorder

## Core Idea
Generalized Anxiety Disorder is characterized by persistent, excessive worry across multiple domains for 6+ months. It reflects dysregulation of threat-detection systems and maladaptive patterns maintaining worry through cognitive and behavioral mechanisms.
