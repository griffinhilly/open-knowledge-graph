# Panic Disorder and Agoraphobia

## Core Idea
Panic Disorder involves recurrent unexpected panic attacks with persistent worry, while agoraphobia reflects avoidance of situations where escape or help may be unavailable. Both maintain through fear-conditioning and safety behaviors.
