# Panic Disorder and Agoraphobia

## Core Idea
Panic Disorder involves recurrent unexpected panic attacks paired with worry about future attacks and their consequences. Agoraphobia develops when individuals avoid situations where escape may be difficult. The fear-avoidance cycle perpetuates the disorder and requires exposure-based intervention.
