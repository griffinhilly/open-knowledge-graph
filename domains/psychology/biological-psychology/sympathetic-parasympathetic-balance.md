# Autonomic Nervous System: Sympathetic and Parasympathetic Balance

## Core Idea
The autonomic nervous system automatically regulates bodily functions through sympathetic (arousing) and parasympathetic (calming) branches. Sympathetic activation increases heart rate, blood pressure, and pupil dilation (fight-or-flight); parasympathetic activation promotes digestion and rest (rest-and-digest). Both branches release neurotransmitters (acetylcholine, norepinephrine) targeting specific receptors on organs. Dysregulation contributes to anxiety disorders.
