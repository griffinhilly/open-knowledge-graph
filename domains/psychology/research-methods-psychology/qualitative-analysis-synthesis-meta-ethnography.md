# Qualitative Research Synthesis and Meta-Ethnography

## Core Idea
Meta-ethnography and qualitative systematic reviews synthesize findings across qualitative studies. Unlike quantitative meta-analysis, qualitative synthesis integrates themes, interpretations, and conceptualizations to develop higher-order syntheses. Transparency in study selection, quality appraisal, and synthesis logic ensure validity.

## How It's Best Learned
Review published qualitative systematic reviews and appraise their synthesis methods. Conceptually synthesize themes from multiple interview studies on a topic. Discuss how qualitative and quantitative synthesis serve different purposes.

## Common Misconceptions
- Qualitative synthesis is informal literature review; - Quality appraisal criteria for RCTs apply unchanged to qualitative research; - Synthesis must aggregate frequencies of codes; - Meta-ethnography requires statistical methods.
