# Bleeding Disorders

## Core Idea
Bleeding disorders result from defects in vascular integrity, platelet function, or coagulation factors. Classification by hemostatic phase reveals pathophysiology: platelet disorders cause mucocutaneous bleeding, and factor deficiencies cause deep tissue or joint bleeding.

## How It's Best Learned
Correlate laboratory findings (bleeding time, PT, aPTT, platelet count) with clinical presentation. Study hemophilia A and B as paradigms of factor X deficiency.

## Common Misconceptions
Low platelet count does not always cause bleeding—function matters more than quantity. Vitamin K deficiency affects multiple factors (II, VII, IX, X), not just one.
