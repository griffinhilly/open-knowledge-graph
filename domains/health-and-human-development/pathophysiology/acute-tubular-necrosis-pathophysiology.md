# Acute Tubular Necrosis: Ischemic and Nephrotoxic Injury to Renal Tubules

## Core Idea
ATN is necrosis and desquamation of tubular epithelial cells, most commonly from ischemia (hypotension, sepsis) or nephrotoxins (aminoglycosides, contrast, myoglobin). Loss of tubular barrier function impairs reabsorption and filtration; recovery depends on surviving progenitor cells repopulating denuded basement membrane.
