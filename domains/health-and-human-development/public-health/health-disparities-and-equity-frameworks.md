# Health Disparities, Equity Frameworks, and Social Determinants

## Core Idea
Health disparities arise from structural inequities in income, education, housing, environmental quality, and healthcare access. These social determinants shape disease burden and outcomes more powerfully than individual behaviors. Public health frameworks distinguish between equity (fair distribution of health) and equality (equal treatment), and emphasize upstream structural changes rather than downstream individual interventions.

## How It's Best Learned
Compare health outcomes (mortality, morbidity) across racial/ethnic groups, socioeconomic strata, and geographic areas, then trace disparities back to underlying structural factors. Examine how policies (housing, transportation, economic) shape health outcomes.

## Common Misconceptions
- Disparities reflect individual lifestyle differences; they reflect historical and ongoing structural discrimination embedded in institutions.
- Addressing disparities requires only culturally tailored programs; without structural change, individual programs have limited impact.
