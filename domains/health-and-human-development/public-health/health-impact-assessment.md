# Health Impact Assessment

## Core Idea
Health impact assessment systematically evaluates how proposed policies, projects, or programs affect population health. HIA identifies intended and unintended health consequences before implementation, supporting evidence-based decision-making in non-health sectors like transportation, housing, urban planning, and economic development. HIA is an upstream intervention that shapes the conditions promoting health.
