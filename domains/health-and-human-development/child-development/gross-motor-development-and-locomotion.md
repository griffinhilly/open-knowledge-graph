# Gross Motor Development and Locomotion

## Core Idea
Gross motor development encompasses the acquisition of large-muscle movements enabling postural control and locomotion: head control (0–3 months), rolling (4–6 months), independent sitting (6–8 months), standing with support (9–12 months), and walking (12–15 months). Development follows cephalocaudal (head to toe) and proximal-distal principles and varies with individual maturation rates, motor opportunity, and cultural movement practices. These capabilities are prerequisites for exploration, independence, and peer play.
