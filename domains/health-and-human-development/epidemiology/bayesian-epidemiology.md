# Bayesian Methods in Epidemiology

## Core Idea
Bayesian analysis combines prior knowledge or beliefs with observed data to update posterior belief in hypotheses or parameter values. Bayesian methods naturally incorporate uncertainty and allow direct probability statements about parameters (unlike frequentist confidence intervals). Prior specification integrates mechanistic knowledge and previous study results. Hierarchical Bayesian models borrow strength across populations or subgroups. Sensitivity analysis tests how posteriors change with different reasonable priors. Bayesian approaches are efficient in low-data settings and multiparameter estimation.

## How It's Best Learned
Conduct Bayesian hypothesis test comparing different exposure effects; show how posterior distributions change under different prior specifications.
