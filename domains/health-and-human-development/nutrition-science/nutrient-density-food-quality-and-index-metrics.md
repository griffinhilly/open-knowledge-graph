# Nutrient Density and Food Quality Metrics

## Core Idea
Nutrient density quantifies nutrient content per unit of energy; nutrient-rich foods provide multiple essential micronutrients and fiber in fewer calories. Indices like the Nutrient-Rich Food Index (NRF) score foods on protective nutrients (vitamins, minerals, protein, fiber) versus limiting nutrients (sodium, added sugar, saturated fat). Energy density—inversely related to nutrient density—predicts body weight trajectories. Population-level selection for higher nutrient-density foods would shift dietary patterns toward greater micronutrient adequacy and improved population health.
