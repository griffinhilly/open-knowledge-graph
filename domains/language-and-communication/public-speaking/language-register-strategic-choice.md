# Language Register and Strategic Choice

## Core Idea
Strategic shifts in language formality—moving between formal and conversational registers—signal different relationships and rhetorical intentions. High formality signals respect and authority; strategic informality signals intimacy and trustworthiness.

## How It's Best Learned
Observe how speakers shift formality levels within a single speech; assess how register changes affect audience perception of credibility and connection. Experiment with the same content delivered in different registers and gather feedback on how the register choice affected understanding.
