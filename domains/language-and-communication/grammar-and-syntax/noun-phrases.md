# Noun Phrases

## Core Idea
A noun phrase consists of a noun and all its modifiers, functioning as a single unit within a sentence. It can serve as a subject, object, complement, or the object of a preposition. Recognizing noun phrases helps learners see how multi-word groups behave as grammatical units.
