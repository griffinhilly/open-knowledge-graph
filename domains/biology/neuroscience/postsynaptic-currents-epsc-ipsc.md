# Postsynaptic Currents: EPSCs and IPSCs

## Core Idea
EPSCs: Na+/Ca2+ inward current through glutamate receptors, depolarizes. IPSCs: Cl− inward through GABA_A, hyperpolarizes. Sum determines firing.
