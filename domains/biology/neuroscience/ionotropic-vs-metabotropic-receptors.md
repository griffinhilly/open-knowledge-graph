# Ionotropic vs. Metabotropic Receptors

## Core Idea
Ionotropic: ligand-gated channels, fast (ms) currents. Metabotropic: G-protein coupled, slow (s-min) modulation. Ionotropic = fast transmission; metabotropic = neuromodulation.
