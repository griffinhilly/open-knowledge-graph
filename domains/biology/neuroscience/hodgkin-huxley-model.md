# Hodgkin-Huxley Model of Action Potentials

## Core Idea
Differential equations describe action potential via voltage-dependent gating variables (m, h, n) for Na+ and K+ channels. Predicts waveform, threshold, refractory period. Foundation of computational neuroscience.
