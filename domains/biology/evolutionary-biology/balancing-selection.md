# Balancing Selection and Maintenance of Variation

## Core Idea
Balancing selection actively maintains genetic polymorphism by preventing fixation of any allele. Mechanisms include heterozygote advantage (heterozygotes have higher fitness), frequency-dependent selection (rare alleles have advantages), and environmental heterogeneity. Balancing selection explains high genetic diversity in natural populations despite strong selection.

## How It's Best Learned
Study examples like sickle-cell heterozygote advantage in malaria-endemic regions and MHC allele diversity in humans. Model frequency-dependent selection mathematically.

## Common Misconceptions
- Polymorphism means selection hasn't acted; balancing selection actively maintains multiple alleles against fixation.
- Heterozygote advantage requires the heterozygote to be the best genotype; frequency-dependent selection maintains polymorphism without heterozygote advantage.
