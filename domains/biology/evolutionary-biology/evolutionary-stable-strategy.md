# Evolutionary Stable Strategy (ESS)

## Core Idea
An evolutionarily stable strategy (ESS) is a strategy that, once established in a population, cannot be invaded by mutant alternatives—it is stable against evolutionary change. An ESS may be pure (one strategy) or mixed (population uses multiple strategies at equilibrium frequencies). Finding ESS solutions predicts behavior and strategies that will persist under evolution.
