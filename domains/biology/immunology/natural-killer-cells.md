# Natural Killer Cell Biology and Function

## Core Idea
Natural killer (NK) cells are innate lymphocytes that kill virus-infected and tumor cells through activating receptor (NKp46, NKG2D) and inhibitory receptor (KIR, NKG2A) signaling. They recognize stressed cells via induced ligands (MICA, MICB, viral peptides) and missing self (low MHC-I expression). NK cells kill via perforin/granzyme and produce IFN-γ to enhance immune responses without requiring prior sensitization.

## How It's Best Learned
Compare NK cell activating vs inhibitory receptors and their ligands. Examine how viral infection or transformation shifts the balance toward activation despite normal MHC-I.

## Common Misconceptions
- NK cells cannot distinguish self from non-self without prior exposure (they use missing-self recognition of MHC-I levels). - NK cells only kill cells (they produce important immunoregulatory cytokines).
