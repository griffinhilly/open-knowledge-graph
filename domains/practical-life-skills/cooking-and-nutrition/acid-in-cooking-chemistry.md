# Acid in Cooking: Chemistry and Balance

## Core Idea
Acids (lemon, vinegar, wine) brighten flavors and cut through richness without adding calories or fat. They slow protein denaturation, keeping cooked foods tender longer. Acid also changes the pH of cooking water, which affects how vegetables soften and how beans cook—acidic water slows cooking, while alkaline water speeds it up.

## How It's Best Learned
Cook the same creamy sauce two ways—one with no acid and one with lemon juice or vinegar added. Taste how the acid makes the dish feel lighter and more interesting. Cook beans in neutral water versus acidic water and observe the texture difference.

## Common Misconceptions
- Acid is only for sour or acidic-tasting dishes. - Adding acid late in cooking is the same as adding it early; timing doesn't change how acid works.
