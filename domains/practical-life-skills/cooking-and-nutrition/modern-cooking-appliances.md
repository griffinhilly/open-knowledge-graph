# Modern Cooking Appliances

## Core Idea
Modern appliances like pressure cookers, air fryers, and slow cookers use different heat transfer mechanisms and require adjusted cooking times and techniques. Pressure and air fryers enable faster cooking; slow cookers enable hands-off preparation. Each excels for different ingredient types and cooking goals.

## How It's Best Learned
Choose one appliance and master it with 5-10 recipes before moving to others. Keep detailed notes on timing, temperature settings, and results.

## Common Misconceptions
Pressure cookers are inherently dangerous; air fryers replace all other cooking methods; slow cooking works only for tough cuts of meat.
