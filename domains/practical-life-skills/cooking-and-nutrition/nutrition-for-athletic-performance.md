# Nutrition for Athletic Performance

## Core Idea
Athletic performance benefits significantly from strategic nutrition—timing carbohydrates and protein around workouts, maintaining proper hydration, and balancing macronutrient ratios for recovery. Pre-workout meals emphasize carbs and some protein; post-workout meals prioritize protein and carbs for muscle repair. Individual needs vary by sport, intensity, and personal goals.

## How It's Best Learned
Experiment with different pre- and post-workout meals and track energy levels, performance metrics, and recovery quality over several weeks.

## Common Misconceptions
High protein alone builds muscle; carbohydrates should be minimized for fitness; supplements replace whole foods.
