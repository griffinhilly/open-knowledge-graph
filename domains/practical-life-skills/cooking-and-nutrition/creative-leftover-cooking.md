# Creative Leftover Cooking

## Core Idea
Leftovers are ingredients for entirely new dishes, not mere reheats. Cooked rice becomes fried rice; roasted vegetables transform into frittatas; shredded meat becomes tacos or salads. Transforming leftovers saves money, reduces waste, and challenges cooking creativity. Understanding which leftovers pair well unlocks endless culinary possibilities.

## How It's Best Learned
Designate one meal per week to cook entirely from leftovers and creative adaptations, documenting successful transformations.

## Common Misconceptions
Leftovers are inherently boring; transformation requires complex recipes; reheating destroys nutrition.
