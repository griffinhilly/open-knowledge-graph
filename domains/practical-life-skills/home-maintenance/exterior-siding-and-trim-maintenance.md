# Exterior Siding, Trim, and Weather Barrier Maintenance

## Core Idea
Exterior siding, trim, caulking, and weatherproofing create a barrier that protects your home's structure from rain, wind, and temperature extremes. Common siding materials include wood, vinyl, brick, and fiber cement, each with specific maintenance needs. Gaps in caulking, damaged trim, and deteriorating siding allow water to enter the home, leading to rot, mold, and structural damage. Regular inspection and maintenance of these weather barriers is far less expensive than structural repairs.
