# Home Maintenance Fundamentals

## Core Idea
Home maintenance is the regular care and repairs needed to keep a house safe, comfortable, and in good condition. It includes preventive tasks (like cleaning gutters to prevent overflow), corrective tasks (fixing problems when they happen), and emergency repairs. Understanding why we maintain our homes helps us plan for repairs and avoid costly damage.

## How It's Best Learned
Observe what needs care or breaks in your own home. Ask homeowners what seasonal tasks they do. Help with basic maintenance to understand the purpose of each task.

## Common Misconceptions
Maintenance is only needed when something breaks. (Preventive maintenance prevents most problems.) Only professionals can do home repairs. (Many basic repairs are safe for homeowners.)
