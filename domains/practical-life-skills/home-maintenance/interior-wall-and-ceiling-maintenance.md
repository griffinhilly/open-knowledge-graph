# Interior Wall and Ceiling Assessment and Repair

## Core Idea
Interior walls and ceilings define your home's living spaces and can be made of drywall, plaster, tile, or wood paneling. These surfaces protect insulation and utilities behind them and indicate the home's condition when they show water stains, cracks, or deterioration. Most interior damage is cosmetic but can indicate underlying problems like water leaks or structural movement. Understanding how to assess damage and perform basic repairs keeps your home looking good and helps identify serious issues early.
