# Understanding Home Systems and How They Work Together

## Core Idea
A home functions through interconnected systems: electrical (power distribution), plumbing (water supply and drainage), HVAC (heating and cooling), structural (foundation and framing), and exterior (roof, siding, windows). Each system has components, maintenance schedules, and failure points. Homeowners who understand how these systems interact can identify problems early, maintain their home effectively, and know when to call a professional.

## How It's Best Learned
Walk through your own home and identify components of each system. Locate your electrical panel, main water shutoff, HVAC unit, and foundation walls. Refer to your home's blueprints or inspection report if available.

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking one system failure won't affect others (e.g., poor drainage affecting foundation).
- Assuming maintenance is only needed when something breaks.
- Not understanding that older systems may have different standards than new building codes.
