# Garden and Planting Basics

## Core Idea
Successful gardening starts with understanding your soil, sun exposure, and hardiness zone — the USDA zone map tells you which plants survive your winters. Amending soil with compost before planting improves drainage in clay soils and water retention in sandy soils. Planting depth, spacing, and sun requirements are printed on seed packets and plant tags and should be followed, not guessed.

## How It's Best Learned
Begin with a small raised bed or container — these provide controlled soil conditions and reduce weeding. Choose plants rated for your hardiness zone and match sun requirements to your actual yard exposure.

## Common Misconceptions
- More water always helps struggling plants — overwatering is the most common cause of houseplant and garden plant death, causing root rot.
- Fertilizer fixes all plant problems — fertilizer feeds healthy plants; it cannot compensate for wrong sun, poor drainage, or disease.
