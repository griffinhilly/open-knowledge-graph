# Home Maintenance Routine and Scheduling

## Core Idea
Consistent preventive maintenance through monthly checks, seasonal tasks, and annual inspections prevents costly emergency repairs and keeps homes functioning well. Creating regular maintenance habits reduces unexpected problems and extends system lifespan.

## How It's Best Learned
Create a checklist based on your home's systems and climate zone. Perform walks through quarterly, complete seasonal tasks before weather changes, and schedule annual inspections. Track what you've done to avoid missing tasks.

## Common Misconceptions
Maintenance is expensive (preventing problems is far cheaper than emergency repairs); you need professionals for everything (many routine tasks are DIY-friendly); annual maintenance is sufficient (monthly and seasonal tasks matter significantly).
