# Wood Trim and Baseboard Installation

## Core Idea
Installing baseboards and trim requires accurate measuring, cutting at proper angles for inside and outside corners, securing with nails or screws into studs, and filling gaps with caulk or wood filler for a finished appearance.

## How It's Best Learned
Start with simple straight runs before tackling corners. Use a miter saw for angle cuts and a stud finder to locate walls for secure fastening. Practice on less visible areas first before prominent rooms.

## Common Misconceptions
All corners are exactly 45-degree angles (real corners are often imperfect); gaps between trim and walls are acceptable (they should be caulked); trim doesn't need to be secured well if it's glued.
