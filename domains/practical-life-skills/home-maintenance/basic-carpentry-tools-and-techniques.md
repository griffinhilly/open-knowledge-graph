# Basic Carpentry Tools and Techniques

## Core Idea
Carpentry in home maintenance involves measuring, cutting, and fastening wood for shelves, trim, and furniture assembly. Essential skills include accurate measuring, straight cuts with hand or power saws, and secure fastening with nails, screws, or wood glue. Safe tool use and proper technique produce straight, level results.
