# Inflation and Purchasing Power Planning

## Core Idea
Inflation erodes the purchasing power of money; at 3% inflation, today's $100 buys only $74 in 10 years. Financial plans must account for inflation's impact on future spending needs, savings targets, real returns, and required asset levels.

## How It's Best Learned
Compare historical prices (college tuition, housing, gas) across decades. Project your future spending needs accounting for 2-3% inflation. Recalculate your emergency fund and financial independence targets.

## Common Misconceptions
Inflation is always 2-3% (it varies significantly); nominal returns are what matters (real returns do); inflation mainly affects retirees; inflation is too unpredictable to plan for.
