# Basic Web Searching Techniques

## Core Idea
Search engines help you find information on the internet. Using clear, specific keywords gets better results than vague searches. Learning to refine searches helps you find exactly what you need.

## How It's Best Learned
Search for a topic with general terms, then search with more specific keywords. Notice how results change. Try putting phrases in quotes to find exact matches.

## Common Misconceptions
- The first result is always most accurate. (First results are most popular or relevant by algorithm, not necessarily most accurate.)
- Searching is free because companies are generous. (Free searches work because companies collect data about you.)
- You can find a clear answer to anything online. (Some things have no clear answer or multiple conflicting perspectives.)
