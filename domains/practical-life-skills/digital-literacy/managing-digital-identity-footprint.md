# Managing Your Digital Identity and Footprint

## Core Idea
Your digital footprint is the trail of information created by your online activities—posts, comments, accounts, and data collected about you. This footprint is searchable and permanent. Managing your digital identity means being intentional about what you share, reviewing platform privacy settings, and understanding what can be found about you. Your digital reputation affects job prospects, relationships, and more.

## How It's Best Learned
Search for yourself on Google and social media platforms. Check privacy settings on your social accounts. Create a plan for what digital identity you want to project.
