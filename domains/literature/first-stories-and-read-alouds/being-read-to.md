# Being Read To

## Core Idea
When a grown-up reads a story out loud, you get to listen, look at the pictures, and imagine what happens next. Being read to is one of the very first ways we experience stories. You hear new words, follow along with the pages, and share the experience with someone you love.

## How It's Best Learned
Sit with a grown-up and listen to a story together. Point at pictures, ask about what is happening, and talk about the story afterward. Try listening to the same story more than once and notice what you remember.

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking you need to read the words yourself for it to count as reading; listening to stories is a powerful form of reading.
- Believing read-alouds are only for very little kids; people of all ages enjoy being read to.
- Assuming you have to sit perfectly still the whole time; it is fine to wiggle, point, and react.

## Explainer

**Being read to** is when someone reads a story out loud to you. A grown-up (like a parent, teacher, or librarian) holds the book and reads the words while you sit and listen. You can look at the **pictures**, watch their face, and hear their voice bringing the story to life. It is one of the most wonderful ways to experience stories.

When someone reads to you, your **imagination** gets to work hard. You are not just hearing words -- you are picturing the characters, imagining what the places look like, and wondering what will happen next. Your brain is busy and happy. Plus, sitting with someone while they read to you feels warm and safe. There is a special closeness about sharing a story together.

Here is something important to know: **listening to stories counts as real reading.** You do not have to read every word yourself with your eyes. When you listen and understand the story, you are learning new words, discovering ideas, and thinking deeply. Your ears and your brain are doing all the work of reading! And you can ask questions, point at pictures, giggle at funny parts, and move around. There is no rule that says you have to sit like a statue.

Being read to is something kids and grown-ups of **all ages** love. Little babies enjoy hearing voices and seeing pictures. Kindergarteners love being read to in **school**, and big kids listen to audiobooks. Even older people enjoy hearing stories! When someone reads to you, you are having an adventure together.
