# Genre Theory in Comparative Context

## Core Idea
Genres operate across cultures (epic, lyric, tragedy, novel) yet take dramatically different formal and functional shapes in different traditions. Comparative genre analysis examines how genres emerge from specific historical conditions while revealing persistent formal concerns—how epic responds to questions of nation and heroism across Homer, Dante, and Brathwaite.
