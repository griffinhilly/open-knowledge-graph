# World Literature Debates: Damrosch, Casanova, Moretti, and Beyond

## Core Idea
Scholars like David Damrosch, Pascale Casanova, and Franco Moretti have developed different theoretical frameworks for understanding world literature. Damrosch emphasizes circulation and reception; Casanova theorizes literary world-systems and prestige economies; Moretti proposes distant reading and quantitative methods. These debates raise fundamental questions: What counts as world literature? How do we compare vastly different traditions? Can we study world literature responsibly without exoticizing or homogenizing difference? Understanding these debates is essential for engaging thoughtfully with comparative literature.
