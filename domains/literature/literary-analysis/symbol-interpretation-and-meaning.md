# Symbolism and Symbolic Meaning in Texts

## Core Idea
Symbols function when concrete objects, actions, or characters come to represent abstract ideas or broader meanings. Analyzing symbolism means recognizing how a symbol operates within a specific text and explaining its significance to the text's overall meaning. Symbols gain power through repetition, context, and cultural association, and their interpretation depends on careful attention to how texts develop them.
