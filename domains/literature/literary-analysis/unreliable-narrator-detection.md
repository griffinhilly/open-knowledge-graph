# Detecting and Analyzing Unreliable Narrators

## Core Idea
Unreliable narrators deceive, misunderstand, or misrepresent events. Detection requires comparing narrator claims against textual evidence and other perspectives. Unreliability is often intentional—authors use it to explore subjectivity, bias, or trauma. Analysis must distinguish between narrator limitation and deliberate deception.
