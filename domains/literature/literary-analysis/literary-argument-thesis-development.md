# Developing Literary Arguments and Theses

## Core Idea
A literary argument makes a specific, debatable claim about a text's meaning or effect. Thesis statements in literary analysis go beyond plot summary to interpret significance. Strong theses stake a claim that readers might reasonably dispute, directing analysis toward textual evidence. Development involves supporting the thesis through careful analysis of textual details.
