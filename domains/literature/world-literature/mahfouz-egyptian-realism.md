# Naguib Mahfouz: Realism and Egyptian Social Narrative

## Core Idea
Naguib Mahfouz (1911-2006) established Arabic-language literary realism through novels depicting Egyptian urban and social life with psychological depth, moral complexity, and historical consciousness. His innovation adapted European realist narrative form to represent Egyptian society, family dynamics, and individual consciousness shaped by colonialism and modernization. Mahfouz's realism incorporates Arabic philosophical and social traditions while engaging European literary forms.

## How It's Best Learned
Study how Mahfouz constructs character psychology and social meaning through detailed observation and narrative voice. Examine how his realism represents Egyptian history and the modernization process.

## Common Misconceptions
Mahfouz's realism is not imitation of European forms—it adapts realist strategies to represent specifically Egyptian experience. His narrative authority incorporates traditional practices alongside modernist psychological realism.
