# Arabian Nights (One Thousand and One Nights): Frame Narrative and Storytelling Craft

## Core Idea
*One Thousand and One Nights* presents an extraordinary frame narrative in which Scheherazade postpones her execution by telling stories each night, each story generating further stories in cascading metanarrative. The tales blend folk narrative, Islamic learning, commercial culture, and philosophical debate, creating a literature that addresses multiple audiences simultaneously while exploring the relationship between storytelling and survival.
