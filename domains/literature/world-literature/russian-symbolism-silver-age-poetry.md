# Russian Symbolism and the Silver Age: Aestheticism and Spiritual Quest

## Core Idea
Russian Symbolism (late 19th-early 20th centuries) created a self-consciously artistic poetry movement emphasizing suggestion, atmosphere, and spiritual transcendence over realist representation. Poets combined European symbolist aesthetics with Russian philosophical and religious concerns, seeking poetry that could access higher truths. The movement influenced literary modernism broadly while maintaining distinctively Russian preoccupations with redemption, apocalypse, and spiritual meaning.
