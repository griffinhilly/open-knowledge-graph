# Contemporary Literary Fiction in the 21st Century

## Core Idea
Contemporary literary fiction navigates globalization, digital communication, climate crisis, and transnational identity, synthesizing postmodern formal innovation with engagement with urgent material conditions. Current literature draws on all preceding movements while creating new forms responsive to contemporary consciousness.
