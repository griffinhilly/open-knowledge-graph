# Literary Realism and Objective Representation

## Core Idea
Literary realism rejected romantic idealization in favor of objective representation of ordinary life, especially middle and working-class experience. Realist authors employed precise observation, psychological depth, and direct representation of social conditions, treating literature as a form of documentary investigation.
