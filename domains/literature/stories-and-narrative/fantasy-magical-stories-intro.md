# Fantasy and Magical Stories: Imaginary Worlds

## Core Idea
Fantasy and magical stories include elements that don't exist in the real world: magic systems, fantastical creatures, enchanted objects, or entirely imaginary worlds. What makes them work is internal consistency—the magical elements follow their own rules, and readers accept those rules as the story's operating system.

## How It's Best Learned
Identify the magical or fantastical elements in a story. How do they work? What are their limits? Are there rules for how magic functions? How does the magical system affect characters' choices and the plot?

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking fantasy is escapist and therefore simple; great fantasy explores complex themes through imaginative frameworks.
- Confusing fantasy with sci-fi; fantasy includes magic and no scientific explanation, sci-fi has extrapolation from science.
- Assuming internal consistency doesn't matter; fantasy requires even stricter logic than realistic fiction to be believable.
