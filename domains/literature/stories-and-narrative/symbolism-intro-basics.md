# Symbols: Objects and Images With Meaning

## Core Idea
A symbol is an object, person, place, or image that represents something abstract or larger than itself. A white dove might symbolize peace, a storm might symbolize inner turmoil, a journey might symbolize growth. Symbols allow authors to communicate complex ideas indirectly and give stories deeper layers of meaning.

## How It's Best Learned
Identify objects or images that repeat in a story and ask: Why does the author keep returning to this? What larger idea might it represent? Does the symbol's meaning change as the story progresses?

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking every object in a story is a symbol; authors include details for many reasons.
- Assuming symbols have universal meanings; the same symbol can mean different things in different stories and cultures.
- Confusing symbol with metaphor; symbols are concrete things that represent ideas, while metaphors make comparisons between different things.
