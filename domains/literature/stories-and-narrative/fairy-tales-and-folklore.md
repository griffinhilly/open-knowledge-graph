# Fairy Tales and Folklore: Traditional Stories

## Core Idea
Fairy tales and folklore are traditional stories passed down through generations, often exploring themes of good versus evil, transformation, magic, and lessons about human nature. They typically feature archetypal characters (innocent hero, wicked witch), clear moral frameworks, and elements of the supernatural. These stories reflect the values and beliefs of their cultures.

## How It's Best Learned
Read several fairy tales or folktales from different cultures. Identify common patterns: recurring character types, moral lessons, magical elements. What themes appear across different cultures' stories?

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking fairy tales are just for children; they explore complex adult themes beneath surface simplicity.
- Confusing fairy tales with fables; fairy tales are longer narratives with developed plots, while fables are brief moral lessons.
- Assuming all fairy tales have happy endings; many traditional versions are darker than modern adaptations.
