# Prose Poetry: Hybrid Form

## Core Idea
Prose poetry abandons the line break, using a prose-like format while retaining poetic compression, imagery, musicality, and emotional intensity. This hybrid form allows poets to use the paragraph's flexibility while maintaining poetry's sensory and emotional power.

## How It's Best Learned
Read collections of prose poetry (e.g., by Charles Simic, Claudia Rankine, Danielle Cadena Deuitch). Notice the paragraph form, sentence syntax, and how imagery and compression function without line breaks. Try converting a lyric poem into prose poetry or vice versa.

## Common Misconceptions
- Prose poetry is just prose with poetic language; it maintains poetic economy, intensity, and imagery while using the paragraph.
- Prose poetry is a compromise or inferior to 'real' poetry; it's a legitimate and increasingly important form.
- Prose poetry must be short; some contemporary prose poetry sequences are quite lengthy.
