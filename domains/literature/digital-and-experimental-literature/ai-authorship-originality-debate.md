# Authorship, Originality, and Artificial Intelligence

## Core Idea
AI-generated literature raises philosophical and legal questions: Is a machine an author? What constitutes originality if the model is trained on human texts? Legal frameworks, literary criticism, and philosophy converge on whether machine agency and creativity are meaningful categories, implicating foundational assumptions about literary authorship.
