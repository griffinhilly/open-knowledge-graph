# Montaigne and the Essay Tradition of Self-Portraiture

## Core Idea
Montaigne invented the modern essay as a form of self-portraiture, using digression and self-examination to explore the human condition through personal reflection on everyday topics. His Essays establish the self as a legitimate subject for literary nonfiction without requiring narrative arc or chronological structure. The Montaignean tradition demonstrates how personal exploration can become philosophical inquiry.
