# Steampunk: Alternate History of Steam-Powered Technology

## Core Idea
Steampunk imagines alternative histories where steam power rather than electricity dominates technology development. Steampunk typically features 19th-century aesthetics (Victorian dress, mechanical clockwork, airships) combined with advanced steam-powered technology, creating anachronistic worlds. The steam-powered retro-future allows exploration of industrial-era themes, colonialism, and class conflict with fantastic, speculative machinery.

## How It's Best Learned
Read Scott Westerfeld's Leviathan trilogy or Cherie Priest's Boneshaker. Notice how the alternate technology creates both narrative possibilities (airships change military strategy) and aesthetic choices.
