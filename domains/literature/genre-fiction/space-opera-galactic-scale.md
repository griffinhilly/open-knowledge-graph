# Space Opera: Epic Adventure on Cosmic Scale

## Core Idea
Space opera features sprawling narratives across multiple worlds, with large-scale conflicts between galactic powers, and grand politics determining human fate. Space opera emphasizes spectacle, heroic action, and emotional drama. It often depicts galactic civilizations with complex political structures, making space opera a vehicle for exploring governance, honor, betrayal, and destiny on the largest possible scale.

## How It's Best Learned
Compare Frank Herbert's Dune with E.E. 'Doc' Smith's Lensman series or contemporary space operas like Expanse. Notice how political intrigue and personal relationships ground the vast scale.
