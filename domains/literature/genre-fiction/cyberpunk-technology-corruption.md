# Cyberpunk: High Technology and Social Decay

## Core Idea
Cyberpunk merges advanced technology with social decay, portraying futures where megacorporations dominate governments and hackers/rebels operate at the margins. Cyberpunk treats technology ambivalently—powerful tools for liberation in marginal hands, but also instruments of surveillance and control. Visual style (neon, digital, worn surfaces) is as important as narrative themes; cyberpunk is as much an aesthetic movement as a literary genre.

## How It's Best Learned
Read William Gibson's Neuromancer (which defined the genre) followed by contemporary cyberpunk exploring how the internet era has changed assumptions. Notice how technology functions as both liberatory and oppressive.

## Common Misconceptions
Cyberpunk is not anti-technology; it's skeptical about who controls technology and who benefits. Cyberpunk can be optimistic if counter-cultural resistance succeeds.
