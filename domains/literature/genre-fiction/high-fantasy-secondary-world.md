# High Fantasy: Fully Realized Secondary Worlds

## Core Idea
High fantasy creates fully realized secondary worlds with their own histories, magic systems, languages, and metaphysics. These secondary worlds operate as complete in themselves, separate from our primary world. High fantasy often features large-scale quests and political struggles across multiple settings, with deep historical backgrounds that lend weight and authenticity to the fictional world.

## How It's Best Learned
Study the appendices to Tolkien's Lord of the Rings alongside a contemporary high fantasy like N.K. Jemisin's Broken Earth trilogy. Notice how secondary worlds establish themselves through language, history, and consistent detail.
