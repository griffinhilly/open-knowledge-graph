# Sacred Space and Cosmography: The Structure of Mythic Worlds

## Core Idea
Sacred space is space invested with spiritual or cosmic significance, often organized hierarchically around a cosmic axis (axis mundi) connecting realms. Myths establish cosmographic models: the position of realms, how they connect, which spaces are accessible or forbidden. Sacred space encodes a culture's understanding of order, boundary, and movement between worlds.

## How It's Best Learned
Map the sacred geography of mythic systems, noting hierarchies, boundaries, and paths between realms. Compare physical geography to mythic cosmography to see how cultures map lived landscape into cosmic structure.

## Common Misconceptions
Sacred space is purely imaginary. (Sacred space often correlates with actual landscapes, temple architecture, and ritual movement.) Cosmic structure is universal. (Cultures organize space radically differently.)
