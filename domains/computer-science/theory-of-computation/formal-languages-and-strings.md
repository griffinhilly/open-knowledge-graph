# Formal Languages and Strings

## Core Idea
A formal language is a set of finite strings (sequences of symbols) over an alphabet. Operations on strings—concatenation, Kleene star, reversal—form the basis for defining language families. The mathematical study of formal languages separates syntax from semantics, enabling precise definitions of what computers recognize and generate.
