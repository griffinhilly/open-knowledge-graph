# Rule of Law and Contract Enforcement

## Core Idea
Rule of law refers to predictable, impartial application of rules and reliable contract enforcement. Reliable courts encourage long-term firm investment; weak judicial systems with corruption restrict firms to informal arrangements with trusted partners, limiting scale and complexity. Strong rule of law environments exhibit higher investment rates and faster structural transformation.
