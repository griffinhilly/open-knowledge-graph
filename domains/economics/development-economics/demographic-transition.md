# The Demographic Transition Model

## Core Idea
The Demographic Transition describes how populations evolve during development: high fertility and mortality give way to low fertility and mortality. Initial demographic dividends (large working-age populations) can boost growth, but without complementary job creation unemployment rises. Later aging creates fiscal pressure. Demographic transitions have profound policy implications for education and pension systems.
