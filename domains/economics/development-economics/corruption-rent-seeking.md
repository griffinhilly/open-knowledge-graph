# Corruption and Rent-Seeking in Development

## Core Idea
Corruption redirects resources from productive investment to rent-seeking (extracting existing wealth rather than creating new wealth). It increases business costs, discourages entrepreneurship, and allocates positions based on connections rather than ability. Natural experiments document that corruption reduces investment, slows growth, and perpetuates poverty; anti-corruption efforts are therefore central to development strategy.
