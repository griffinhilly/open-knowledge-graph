# Capital Flows and Balance of Payments

## Core Idea
The balance of payments records a country's international transactions: the current account (trade in goods and services plus income flows) and the capital account (flows of foreign investment). Countries with current account deficits attract foreign capital inflows, financing either higher domestic investment or consumption. Long-term sustainability requires that persistent current account deficits eventually reverse or that foreign debt levels remain manageable relative to income and collateral.
