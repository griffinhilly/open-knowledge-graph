# Real Business Cycle Theory

## Core Idea
RBC theory explains business cycle fluctuations as rational responses to real productivity shocks in market-clearing equilibrium. Agents maximize lifetime utility facing Euler equations; productivity variations cause swings in investment and labor supply that resemble empirical business cycles without monetary frictions.
