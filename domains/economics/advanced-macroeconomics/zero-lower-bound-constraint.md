# Zero Lower Bound and Monetary Policy Constraints

## Core Idea
When nominal interest rates hit zero, conventional monetary policy cannot lower rates further. At the ZLB, central banks rely on forward guidance and balance-sheet policies (QE). The existence of the ZLB constraint changes optimal policy: pre-emptive action during normal times reduces the risk of hitting zero when future shocks occur.
