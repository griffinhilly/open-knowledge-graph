# First Welfare Theorem (Efficiency of Competition)

## Core Idea
The First Welfare Theorem states that every Walrasian equilibrium allocation is Pareto efficient. Under perfect competition, self-interest and profit-seeking lead to an allocation where no one can improve without hurting others. This justifies the notion of invisible hand and provides a moral argument for competitive markets.

## How It's Best Learned
Prove the theorem for a simple exchange economy. Identify where convexity assumptions matter. Discuss limitations: market failures, public goods, externalities violate assumptions.
