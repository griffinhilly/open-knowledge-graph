# Walrasian (Competitive) Equilibrium

## Core Idea
A Walrasian equilibrium is a price vector p* and allocation (x*, y*) such that: (1) consumers maximize utility at p*, (2) firms maximize profit at p*, and (3) all markets clear (supply equals demand). Named after Léon Walras, it formalizes the idea of perfectly competitive equilibrium and is the central concept in general equilibrium theory.

## How It's Best Learned
Begin with partial equilibrium (single market clearing). Then study equilibrium in a two-good, two-consumer exchange economy using the Edgeworth box. Graduate to many-goods production economies. Verify clearing in excess demand form.
