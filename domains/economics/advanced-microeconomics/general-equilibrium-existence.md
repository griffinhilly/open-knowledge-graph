# Existence of General Equilibrium

## Core Idea
The Arrow-Debreu-McKenzie model proves existence of Walrasian equilibrium under mild convexity and continuity conditions. The proof uses Brouwer's fixed point theorem: excess demand mappings have fixed points where supply equals demand. This is a foundational result in mathematical economics and justifies the study of equilibrium.
