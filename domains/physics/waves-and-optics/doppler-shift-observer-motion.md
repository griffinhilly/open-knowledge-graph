# Doppler Effect: Observer in Motion

## Core Idea
When an observer moves toward a stationary sound source at speed vo, the observed frequency increases: f' = f(1 + vo/v). When moving away, frequency decreases: f' = f(1 - vo/v). The observer effectively samples wave crests at a faster rate when approaching, leading to higher apparent frequency.
