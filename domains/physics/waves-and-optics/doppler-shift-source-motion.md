# Doppler Effect: Source in Motion

## Core Idea
When a sound source moves toward a stationary observer at speed vs, the wavelength compresses and observed frequency increases: f' = f/(1 - vs/v). When moving away, frequency decreases: f' = f/(1 + vs/v). The moving source effectively 'bunches up' wave crests in the forward direction, increasing frequency ahead of the source.
