# Equipotential Surfaces and Field Geometry

## Core Idea
Equipotential surfaces are loci of constant potential V = const. Electric field lines are always perpendicular to equipotential surfaces and point from high to low potential. The density of equipotential surfaces indicates field strength. These surfaces are crucial for visualizing and understanding field geometry and for solving boundary value problems.
