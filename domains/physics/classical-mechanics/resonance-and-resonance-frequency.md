# Resonance and Resonance Frequency

## Core Idea
Resonance occurs when a system is driven near its natural frequency, producing large-amplitude oscillations. In damped systems, the resonance frequency is slightly lower than the natural frequency, and the peak amplitude is inversely related to damping strength. This is fundamental to understanding vibration in mechanical systems.
