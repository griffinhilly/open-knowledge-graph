# Vis-Viva Equation

## Core Idea
The vis-viva equation v² = GM(2/r - 1/a) relates orbital speed to position and semi-major axis. At any given distance, the speed depends only on the semi-major axis, allowing rapid calculation of speeds at any orbital point without solving the full trajectory.
