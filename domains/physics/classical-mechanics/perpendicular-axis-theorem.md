# Perpendicular Axis Theorem

## Core Idea
For a planar laminar (thin 2D object), the moment of inertia about an axis perpendicular to the plane equals the sum of moments of inertia about two perpendicular axes in the plane: I_z = I_x + I_y. This relationship is useful for calculating moments of inertia of flat objects about various axes.
