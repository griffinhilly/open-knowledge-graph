# Instantaneous Center of Rotation

## Core Idea
For any rigid body in planar motion at a given instant, there exists a unique instantaneous center of rotation (ICR) about which the body appears to be purely rotating. This geometric concept simplifies analysis by reducing complex motion to pure rotation about a moving point, valid for that instant only.
