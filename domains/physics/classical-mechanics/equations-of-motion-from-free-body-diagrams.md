# Equations of Motion from Free-Body Diagrams

## Core Idea
By systematically drawing free-body diagrams and applying Newton's second law to each object, we can derive the equations of motion for any mechanical system. This formal approach handles simple and complex systems uniformly, including constraints like normal forces and tension.
