# Center of Mass Motion

## Core Idea
For any system of particles, the center of mass moves as if all mass were concentrated there and all external forces applied to it. This separation principle allows decomposition into center-of-mass motion and internal motion, dramatically simplifying multi-particle dynamics by decoupling overall translation from internal structure.
