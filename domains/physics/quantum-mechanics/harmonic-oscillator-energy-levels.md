# Energy Levels and Eigenstates of the Quantum Harmonic Oscillator

## Core Idea
Eigenstates |n⟩ with energies E_n = (n + ½)ℏω form an orthonormal basis. The spectrum is equally spaced—unique to quadratic potentials.
