# Born Approximation

## Core Idea
In the Born approximation, the scattering amplitude is f(q) ≈ -(m/2πℏ²) ∫ V(r) e^{iq·r} d³r, where q is momentum transfer. Valid when |V| ≪ ℏ²k²/m, it connects the potential directly to the cross section.
