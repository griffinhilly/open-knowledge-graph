# The Born Approximation in Scattering

## Core Idea
Born approximation: f(θ) ≈ −(m/2πℏ²) ∫ e^{iq·r'} V(r') d³r' with momentum transfer q. Valid for weak scattering or high energy. Predicts Rutherford scattering.
