# Pauli Matrices

## Core Idea
The Pauli matrices σₓ, σᵧ, σz are 2×2 matrices representing spin-½ operators via Ŝᵢ = (ℏ/2)σᵢ. They satisfy σᵢσⱼ = δᵢⱼ + iεᵢⱼₖσₖ and form a basis for all 2×2 matrices, encoding the complete structure of spin-½.
