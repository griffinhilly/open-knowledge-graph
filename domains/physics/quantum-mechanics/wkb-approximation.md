# The WKB Approximation

## Core Idea
WKB is a semiclassical method valid when de Broglie wavelength varies slowly. Writing ψ(x) ≈ A(x) e^{iS(x)/ℏ} accurately describes tunneling, quantization, and smooth-potential scattering.
