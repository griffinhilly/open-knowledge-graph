# Quantum Entanglement

## Core Idea
An entangled state cannot be written as |ψ₁⟩⊗|ψ₂⟩. The Bell state |Φ⁺⟩ = (|↑↓⟩ + |↓↑⟩)/√2 is entangled: measuring one spin constrains the other regardless of distance.
