# Variational Method

## Core Idea
The variational method states that for any trial state |ψ(α)⟩: ⟨ψ|H|ψ⟩/⟨ψ|ψ⟩ ≥ E₀. Minimizing over variational parameters α gives an upper bound on ground state energy, useful when exact solutions are impossible.
