# The Variational Principle in Quantum Mechanics

## Core Idea
For any normalized |ψ⟩, ⟨ψ|Ĥ|ψ⟩ ≥ E₀ (ground state energy). Minimizing over trial wavefunctions estimates E₀ without solving Schrödinger's equation exactly.
