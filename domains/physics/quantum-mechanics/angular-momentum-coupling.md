# Angular Momentum Coupling

## Core Idea
When two angular momenta couple, J⃗ = J⃗₁ + J⃗₂ has quantum number j ranging from |j₁ − j₂| to j₁ + j₂. The basis transformation uses Clebsch-Gordan coefficients.
