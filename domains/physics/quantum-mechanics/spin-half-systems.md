# Spin-1/2 Systems

## Core Idea
Electrons and nucleons have intrinsic angular momentum (spin) with s = ½, giving two possible z-components: m_s = ±½. The spin-½ system is the simplest nontrivial quantum system with a 2-dimensional Hilbert space.
