# Pure States and Mixed States

## Core Idea
A pure state |ψ⟩ has ρ = |ψ⟩⟨ψ| with Tr(ρ²) = 1. A mixed state has Tr(ρ²) < 1, representing loss of information due to decoherence or measurement.
