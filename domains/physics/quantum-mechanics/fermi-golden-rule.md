# The Fermi Golden Rule

## Core Idea
Transition rate to continuum: Γ_{i→f} = (2π/ℏ) |⟨f|H'|i⟩|² ρ(E_f), where ρ(E_f) is the density of final states. Predicts absorption, emission, decay, and scattering rates.
