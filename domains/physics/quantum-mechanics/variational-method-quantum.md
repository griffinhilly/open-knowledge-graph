# The Variational Method: Application

## Core Idea
Parameterize trial wavefunction ψ(α, β, ...) and compute ⟨H⟩; minimize by ∂⟨H⟩/∂α = 0. Widely used in quantum chemistry (Hartree-Fock) and condensed matter.
