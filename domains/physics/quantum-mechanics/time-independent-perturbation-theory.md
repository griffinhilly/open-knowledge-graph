# Time-Independent Perturbation Theory

## Core Idea
For a solvable unperturbed Hamiltonian H₀ with small perturbation H', energies and states are power series expansions: E = E⁽⁰⟩ + λE⁽¹⟩ + ...., |ψ⟩ = |ψ⁽⁰⟩⟩ + λ|ψ⁽¹⟩⟩ + ....
