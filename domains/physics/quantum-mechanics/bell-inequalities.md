# Bell Inequalities and Their Violation

## Core Idea
CHSH inequality: |⟨AB⟩ + ⟨AB'⟩ + ⟨A'B⟩ − ⟨A'B'⟩| ≤ 2 in local hidden-variable theories. Quantum mechanics allows ≤ 2√2 for entangled states.
