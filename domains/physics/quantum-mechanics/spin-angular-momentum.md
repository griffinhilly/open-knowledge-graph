# Spin Angular Momentum

## Core Idea
Spin is intrinsic angular momentum with no classical analog. Electrons have s = ½, obeying [Ŝ_i, Ŝ_j] = iℏ ε_{ijk} Ŝ_k. The spin magnetic moment couples to magnetic fields.
