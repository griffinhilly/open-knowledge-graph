# Spin Angular Momentum

## Core Idea
Spin S̄ is intrinsic angular momentum independent of orbital motion, with the same commutation relations as orbital angular momentum but without a classical analogue. For electrons (and other fermions), the spin quantum number is s = 1/2, giving mₛ = ±1/2 and total spin magnitude ℏ√(3/4). Spin is described by the Pauli matrices with S̄ = (ℏ/2)σ̄.
