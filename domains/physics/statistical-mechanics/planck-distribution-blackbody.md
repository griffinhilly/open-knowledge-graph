# Planck Distribution and Blackbody Radiation

## Core Idea
Photons in thermal equilibrium obey Bose-Einstein statistics. The Planck distribution n(ω) = 1/(exp(ℏω/kT) - 1) gives the average number of photons at frequency ω. Integrating over all frequencies yields total energy, entropy, and pressure of radiation. The T⁴ behavior of radiated power (Stefan-Boltzmann law) emerges naturally from statistical mechanics.
