# The Grand Canonical Ensemble (Fixed T, μ, V)

## Core Idea
The grand canonical ensemble allows both energy and particle number to fluctuate; temperature T, chemical potential μ, and volume V are fixed. Microstates have probability proportional to exp(-(E_i - μN_i)/kT). This ensemble is invaluable for systems with particle creation/annihilation and for describing quantum gases with varying particle numbers.
