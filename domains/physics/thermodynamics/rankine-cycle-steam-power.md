# The Rankine Cycle and Steam Power Plants

## Core Idea
The Rankine cycle consists of isobaric vaporization in a boiler, isentropic expansion in a turbine, isobaric condensation in a condenser, and isentropic compression in a pump. It models conventional steam power plants. Efficiency depends on boiler and condenser pressures and superheat. Real Rankine plants achieve 30-40% efficiency.
