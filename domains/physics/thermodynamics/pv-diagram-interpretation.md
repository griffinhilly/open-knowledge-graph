# P-V Diagrams and Thermodynamic Process Representation

## Core Idea
P-V diagrams show process paths and enable graphical calculation of work: the area under the curve is ∫P dV = W_expansion. Horizontal lines represent isobaric, vertical lines represent isochoric, hyperbolas represent isothermal, and exponentials represent adiabatic processes. Closed cycles show net work as the enclosed area.
