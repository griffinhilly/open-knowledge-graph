# The Van der Waals Equation of State

## Core Idea
The van der Waals equation (P + a(n/V)²)(V - nb) = nRT adds two corrections to the ideal gas law: 'a' accounts for intermolecular attraction (reducing pressure), and 'b' accounts for excluded molecular volume (reducing available volume). It predicts critical behavior and phase transitions, reducing to the ideal gas law as a→0, b→0.
