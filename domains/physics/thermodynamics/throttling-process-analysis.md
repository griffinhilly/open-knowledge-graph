# Throttling Processes: Isenthalpic Flow and Applications

## Core Idea
Throttling is an isenthalpic (constant H) expansion across a restriction with no shaft work or significant heat transfer. For real gases, T and S change during throttling even though H remains constant. Throttling is irreversible: entropy always increases. On a Mollier (H-S) diagram, throttling appears as a vertical line.
