# Polytropic Processes and the Polytropic Index

## Core Idea
A polytropic process follows PV^n = constant for an ideal gas, where n is the polytropic index. n=1 (isothermal), n=γ (adiabatic), n=0 (isobaric), and n=∞ (isochoric) are special cases. Real processes with friction have n between 1 and γ. Work and heat transfer depend on n: W = (P₂V₂ - P₁V₁)/(1-n).
