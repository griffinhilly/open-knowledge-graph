# The Brayton Cycle and Gas Turbine Engines

## Core Idea
The Brayton cycle consists of two isobaric processes (heat addition and rejection) and two adiabatic processes (compression and expansion). It models gas turbines and jet engines. Efficiency is η = 1 - 1/r_p^((γ-1)/γ), where r_p = P₂/P₁ is the pressure ratio. Brayton efficiency increases directly with pressure ratio.
