# Thermodynamic Availability and Exergy Analysis

## Core Idea
Exergy (available work) is the maximum useful work extractable from a system relative to surroundings at T₀, P₀. It is zero at dead state (equilibrium with surroundings). For a closed system: Ex = (U - U₀) - T₀(S - S₀) + P₀(V - V₀). Exergy is destroyed in irreversible processes; reversible processes have zero exergy destruction.
