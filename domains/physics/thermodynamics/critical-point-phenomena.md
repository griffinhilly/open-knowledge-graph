# Critical Point Phenomena and Equation-of-State Behavior

## Core Idea
At the critical point (Tc, Pc, ρc), the liquid-gas distinction disappears: (∂P/∂V)_T = 0 and (∂²P/∂V²)_T = 0. Near the critical point, properties diverge with universal critical exponents. The van der Waals equation predicts critical behavior qualitatively but underestimates exponent magnitudes.
