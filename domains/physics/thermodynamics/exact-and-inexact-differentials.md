# Exact and Inexact Differentials in Thermodynamics

## Core Idea
A differential dU is exact if ∫ dU is path-independent; mathematically, mixed partial derivatives must be equal: (∂²U/∂x∂y) = (∂²U/∂y∂x). For inexact differentials like δQ and δW, mixed partials are unequal. Only exact differentials represent state functions. The integrating factor 1/T converts inexact δQ to exact dS for reversible processes.
