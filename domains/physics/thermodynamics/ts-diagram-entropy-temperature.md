# T-S Diagrams and Entropy-Temperature Representation

## Core Idea
T-S diagrams display thermodynamic cycles with temperature on the vertical axis and entropy on the horizontal axis. Area under a curve represents heat: Q = ∫T dS. Reversible processes appear as distinct curves; irreversible processes show entropy increase at constant temperature. T-S diagrams are particularly useful for analyzing steam cycles.
