# Real Gas Deviations from Ideality

## Core Idea
Real gases deviate from PV = nRT due to intermolecular attractions and molecular size. The compressibility factor Z = PV/nRT ≠ 1 for real gases. Deviations are strongest near liquefaction (high pressure, low temperature) and are quantified by virial coefficients B(T), C(T), ... that depend on temperature and molecular properties.
