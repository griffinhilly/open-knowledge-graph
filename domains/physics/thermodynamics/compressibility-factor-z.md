# Compressibility Factor and Real Gas Corrections

## Core Idea
The compressibility factor Z = PV/nRT measures deviation from ideality: Z=1 for ideal gases, Z<1 when attractions dominate, Z>1 when repulsion dominates. For van der Waals gas, Z ≈ 1 + B'P/RT where B' = (b - a/RT). Generalized Z charts allow estimation for many substances across wide conditions.
