# The Diesel Cycle and Compression-Ignition Engines

## Core Idea
The Diesel cycle consists of adiabatic compression, isobaric heat addition, adiabatic expansion, and isochoric heat rejection. It models compression-ignition engines. Efficiency depends on compression ratio and expansion ratio. Diesel engines achieve higher efficiency than Otto at the same compression ratio due to isobaric instead of isochoric heat addition.
