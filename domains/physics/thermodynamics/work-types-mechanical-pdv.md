# Types of Thermodynamic Work: Expansion, Electrical, and Others

## Core Idea
While P-dV expansion work W_exp = ∫P dV dominates, thermodynamic work includes electrical work (W_elec = φ dq), surface work (W_surf = γ dA), magnetic work, and shaft work. The first law dU = δQ - δW must account for all forms. Different processes involve different work types.
