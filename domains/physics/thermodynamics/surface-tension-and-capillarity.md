# Surface Tension, Surface Energy, and Capillary Phenomena

## Core Idea
Surface tension γ = (∂G/∂A)_{T,P} is the Gibbs energy cost of creating unit area. Surface energy drives capillary rise in narrow tubes and affects bubble/droplet equilibrium. Young's equation γ_SV - γ_SL = γ cos θ relates contact angle to surface tensions. Interfacial thermodynamics governs emulsions and colloidal stability.
