# Spatial Layout and Geometry

## Core Idea

An innate core-knowledge system for space: a sense of distance, direction, and the arrangement of
places, used to navigate and to remember where things are. It carries an early sensitivity to shape
and geometric relationships, and it is the substrate beneath positional language (in, on, under,
beside), shape recognition, and eventually formal geometry.

## Emerges Through

Built through moving the body through the world and acting on space: crawling around furniture,
fitting a shape into a sorter, finding a hidden toy by where it was left, climbing in and out of
boxes. Caregivers reinforce it with simple spatial play and with words for where things are.

## Observable Signs

Illustrative, not a checklist:

- Finding a familiar object by remembering roughly where it is in a room.
- Fitting things into containers and openings, adjusting for shape and size.
- Orienting toward a destination across a space rather than wandering at random.

Developmental markers vary widely; this is an enrichment map, not a screening tool. For concerns
about a child's development, consult a pediatrician.
