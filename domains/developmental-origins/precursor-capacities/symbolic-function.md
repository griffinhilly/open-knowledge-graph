# Symbolic Thinking

## Core Idea

The gateway capacity: holding something in mind in its absence and letting one thing stand for
another. It is what makes a banana become a telephone in play, what lets a word, drawing, or number
represent a thing that is not here. Once symbolic function is online, language, pretend play, counting,
and the whole pre-formal curriculum become learnable. It rests on object permanence (holding in mind
what is out of sight) and on social partnership (symbols are shared with someone — joint attention);
it in turn grounds naming, sharpens discernment, and forms the bridge from the origin layer up into
taught topics.

## Emerges Through

Built through pretend and representation: feeding a doll, using a block as a car, scribbling to "write,"
recalling and acting out something seen earlier. Pretend play, storybooks, and drawing all exercise the
move from a thing to a stand-in for it.

## Observable Signs

Illustrative, not a checklist:

- Pretend play — using one object as if it were another, or acting out a remembered scene.
- Treating a picture, word, or gesture as standing for a real thing.
- Recalling and re-enacting something seen earlier, without it being present.

Developmental markers vary widely; this is an enrichment map, not a screening tool. For concerns
about a child's development, consult a pediatrician.
