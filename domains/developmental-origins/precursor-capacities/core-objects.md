# Object Cohesion and Permanence

## Core Idea

One of the innate core-knowledge systems: the expectation that the world is made of bounded, solid
things that move as connected wholes, cannot pass through one another, and keep existing when out of
sight. This is the substrate that lets counting individuate "things," that lets hide-and-seek and
peekaboo make sense, and that anchors the idea of a stable world to reason about at all.

## Emerges Through

This system is present very early and is sharpened through ordinary handling of the world: tracking a
rolling ball behind a couch, watching a toy disappear under a blanket and reaching for it, stacking
and knocking down. Caregivers strengthen it simply by playing peekaboo and by letting a child act on
objects that occlude, contain, and reappear.

## Observable Signs

Illustrative, not a checklist:

- Searching for a toy that has rolled out of view, rather than treating it as gone.
- Enjoying peekaboo and games where something hides and returns.
- Acting surprised when an object seems to vanish or pass through a barrier.

Developmental markers vary widely; this is an enrichment map, not a screening tool. For concerns
about a child's development, consult a pediatrician.
