# Combining Small Groups (Pre-Addition)

## Core Idea
Combining small groups means putting two collections together and finding how many there are in all. This is the conceptual foundation of addition before formal notation is introduced. Children see that combining a group of 3 and a group of 2 results in a group of 5.

## How It's Best Learned
Use physical objects: 'Put 3 frogs in the pond, then add 2 more. Count how many altogether.' Use story contexts to make combining meaningful before introducing the + symbol.

## Common Misconceptions
- Counting only the second group instead of the whole combined group.
- Forgetting the original count and starting over when the groups are combined.
