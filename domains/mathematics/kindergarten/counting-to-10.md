# Counting to 10

## Core Idea
Counting to 10 means saying the number names in the correct order: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. Each number represents a quantity one greater than the one before it. This is the foundation of all number sense.

## How It's Best Learned
Use songs, finger counting, and physical objects. Children should touch or move objects one at a time as they say each number. Repetition through daily routines (counting steps, snacks, etc.) builds automaticity.

## Common Misconceptions
- Skipping numbers (e.g., '1, 2, 3, 5') — emphasize consistent order.
- Reciting the sequence without understanding that each word refers to a quantity.
