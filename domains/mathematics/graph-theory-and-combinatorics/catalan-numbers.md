# Catalan Numbers and Applications

## Core Idea
Catalan numbers C_n = (1/(n+1))(2n choose n) count many combinatorial structures: balanced parentheses, binary trees, Dyck paths, triangulations. The exponential generating function and recurrence relations reveal why these seemingly unrelated objects share the same count.
