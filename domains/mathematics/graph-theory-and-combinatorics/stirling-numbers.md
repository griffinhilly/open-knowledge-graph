# Stirling Numbers of the First and Second Kind

## Core Idea
Stirling numbers of the second kind S(n,k) count partitions of an n-set into k non-empty subsets. Stirling numbers of the first kind s(n,k) count permutations of n elements with exactly k cycles. Both satisfy recurrence relations and connect to falling/rising factorials and polynomial expansions.
