# The Lovász Local Lemma

## Core Idea
The Lovász Local Lemma provides a sufficient condition for the existence of a configuration avoiding a family of 'bad' events, even when events are weakly dependent. This powerful tool extends the probabilistic method to settings where simple union bound arguments fail.
