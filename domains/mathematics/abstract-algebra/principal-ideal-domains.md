# Principal Ideal Domains

## Core Idea
A principal ideal domain (PID) is an integral domain where every ideal is principal, generated by a single element. Euclidean domains are PIDs. In PIDs, gcd and lcm are well-defined and Bézout's identity holds: gcd(a,b) is a linear combination of a and b.
