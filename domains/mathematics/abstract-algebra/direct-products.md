# Direct Products

## Core Idea
The direct product G × H has elements (g, h) with operation (g₁, h₁)(g₂, h₂) = (g₁g₂, h₁h₂). G × H is abelian iff both factors are. The order is |G| · |H| and direct products are commutative: G × H ≅ H × G.
