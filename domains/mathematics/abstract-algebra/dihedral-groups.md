# Dihedral Groups

## Core Idea
The dihedral group D_n is the symmetry group of a regular n-gon with 2n elements: n rotations and n reflections. D_n is non-abelian for n ≥ 3 and has presentation ⟨r, s | r^n = s^2 = e, srs = r^{-1}⟩. Dihedral groups are canonical non-abelian examples.
