# De Morgan's Laws

## Core Idea
De Morgan's Laws state that ¬(p ∧ q) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q and ¬(p ∨ q) ≡ ¬p ∧ ¬q. These laws describe how negation distributes over AND and OR, essential for manipulating logical expressions.
