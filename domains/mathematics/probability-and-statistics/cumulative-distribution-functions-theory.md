# Cumulative Distribution Functions

## Core Idea
The CDF F(x)=P(X≤x) is non-decreasing with F(−∞)=0 and F(∞)=1, defined for all random variables. For continuous X, f(x)=F'(x). CDFs enable computing tail probabilities and quantiles, and completely characterize any distribution.
