# Flux Integrals of Vector Fields

## Core Idea
The flux of F through surface S: ∬_S F · dS = ∬_S F · n dS, where n is the unit normal. If S is parametrized, ∬_S F · dS = ∬_D F · (r_u × r_v) du dv. Flux measures flow rate through the surface.
