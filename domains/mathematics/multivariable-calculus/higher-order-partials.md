# Higher-Order Partial Derivatives

## Core Idea
Higher-order partials are partial derivatives of partial derivatives: ∂²f/∂x², ∂²f/∂y², and mixed partials ∂²f/∂x∂y. Notation ∂²f/∂x∂y means first differentiate with respect to y, then x.
