# Multiplication Facts: 6s Through 9s

## Core Idea
Multiplication facts for 6, 7, 8, and 9 extend the model of equal groups and arrays. These are typically the hardest facts for students to automatize, but pattern recognition and prior knowledge of smaller facts help.

## How It's Best Learned
Use known facts to derive unknowns (e.g., 6 × 7 = 5 × 7 + 7). Practice with manipulatives first, then move to mental strategies. Distribute practice over multiple weeks with regular review of easier facts.

## Common Misconceptions
- Mixing up 6 × 7 and 7 × 6 in terms of which one you've memorized.
- Relying solely on counting by ones rather than using strategies.
- Forgetting facts that haven't been reviewed regularly.
