# Fractions: Sixths and Eighths

## Core Idea
Sixths and eighths extend fraction understanding beyond halves, thirds, and fourths. A sixth is one of six equal parts (1/6); an eighth is one of eight equal parts (1/8). Students practice reading, writing, and representing fractions with denominators 6 and 8, including non-unit fractions like 3/6 or 5/8.

## How It's Best Learned
Folded paper strips work especially well — fold in half three times to get eighths. Number lines divided into sixths or eighths help students see where fractions fall relative to 0 and 1.

## Common Misconceptions
- Students may not realize that folding a strip already folded in thirds in half again creates sixths.
- Writing fractions: students sometimes reverse numerator and denominator when writing.
