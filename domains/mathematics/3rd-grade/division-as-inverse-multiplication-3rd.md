# Division as the Inverse of Multiplication

## Core Idea
Multiplication and division are inverse operations. If 3 × 4 = 12, then 12 ÷ 3 = 4 and 12 ÷ 4 = 3. Division 'undoes' multiplication.

## How It's Best Learned
Use fact families: 3, 4, 12 creates four related facts (3 × 4, 4 × 3, 12 ÷ 3, 12 ÷ 4).

## Common Misconceptions
Not seeing the connection; confusing which number is the divisor.
