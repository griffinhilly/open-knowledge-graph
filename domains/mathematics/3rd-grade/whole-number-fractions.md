# Fractions Equal to Whole Numbers

## Core Idea
Some fractions equal whole numbers: 2/2 = 1, 4/4 = 1, 6/3 = 2, 8/4 = 2, and 0/4 = 0. When the numerator is equal to the denominator, the fraction equals 1. When the numerator is a multiple of the denominator, the fraction equals a whole number. Recognizing these equalities deepens fraction sense and prepares students for improper fractions.

## How It's Best Learned
Use number lines marked in thirds or fourths and have students hop from 0 to see that 3/3 lands exactly on 1 and 6/3 lands exactly on 2. Fraction bar models can also show that 4/4 fills a complete whole.

## Common Misconceptions
- Students may think a fraction must always be less than 1.
- They may not recognize 8/4 = 2 without calculating, treating it as an unusual or incorrect fraction.
