# Multi-Step Word Problems

## Core Idea
Multi-step word problems require two or more operations to solve. Students read the problem carefully, identify what is known and unknown, decide which operations to use and in what order, and compute step by step. Problems at this level mix addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division in realistic contexts.

## How It's Best Learned
Teach a structured read-draw-solve-check routine. Tape diagrams or bar models help students plan the solution before computing. Start with problems that explicitly signal the steps, then move to problems where students must identify the steps themselves.

## Common Misconceptions
- Students solve only the first step and stop, forgetting there is a second question.
- Choosing the wrong operation because they misread what the problem is asking.
- Not labeling intermediate and final answers, leading to confusion about what each number represents.
