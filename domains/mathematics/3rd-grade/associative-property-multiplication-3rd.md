# Associative Property of Multiplication

## Core Idea
When multiplying three factors, grouping doesn't matter: (2 × 3) × 4 = 2 × (3 × 4) = 24. This helps simplify calculations.

## How It's Best Learned
Use arrays grouped different ways to show the same product.

## Common Misconceptions
Confusing with commutativity; thinking grouping changes the answer.
