# Odd and Even Numbers and Patterns

## Core Idea
Even numbers are divisible by 2 (0, 2, 4, 6, 8...); odd numbers have a remainder of 1 when divided by 2 (1, 3, 5, 7, 9...). Patterns emerge: even + even = even, odd + odd = even, even + odd = odd.
