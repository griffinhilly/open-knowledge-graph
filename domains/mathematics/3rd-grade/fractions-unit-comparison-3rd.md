# Comparing Unit Fractions

## Core Idea
Unit fractions have a numerator of 1. As the denominator increases, the unit fraction gets smaller (1/2 > 1/3 > 1/4). Comparing requires understanding that more equal parts means each part is smaller.

## How It's Best Learned
Use pie charts or area models (folded paper, drawings) to show unit fractions visually. Compare side-by-side. Arrange unit fractions on a number line and notice the pattern.

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking 1/4 is larger than 1/3 because 4 is larger than 3.
- Confusing the denominator with the size of the part (higher denominator = bigger piece).
