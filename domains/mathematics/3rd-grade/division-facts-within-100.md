# Division Facts Within 100

## Core Idea
Division facts within 100 are the quotients of any product within 100 divided by a single-digit divisor: 56÷7=8, 81÷9=9, and so on. Because every division fact is the reverse of a multiplication fact, students who know their times tables can derive division facts immediately. The goal is automatic retrieval.

## How It's Best Learned
Teach division facts in the same family groups as multiplication. If students know 6×8=48, they should instantly derive 48÷6=8 and 48÷8=6. Practice with fact family triangles and related sets.

## Common Misconceptions
- Students may not realize that division facts are embedded in multiplication facts — they try to memorize division separately.
- Division by 1 (any number ÷ 1 = itself) and a number by itself (n ÷ n = 1) are frequently miscalculated.
