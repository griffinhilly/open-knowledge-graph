# Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (Rigorous)

## Core Idea
The Fundamental Theorem has two parts: (1) If f is continuous on [a, b], then F(x) = ∫_a^x f is differentiable with F'(x) = f(x). (2) If f is integrable and has an antiderivative F on [a, b], then ∫_a^b f = F(b) − F(a). These connect differentiation and integration as inverse operations and are central to all of calculus.
