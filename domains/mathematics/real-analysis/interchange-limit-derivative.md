# Interchange of Limit and Derivative

## Core Idea
If (fₙ) is a sequence of differentiable functions such that (fₙ') converges uniformly to g and (fₙ) converges pointwise to f, then f is differentiable and f' = g. That is, (lim fₙ)' = lim (fₙ'). The uniform convergence of derivatives is necessary; without it, limits of differentiable functions can be non-differentiable.
