# Absolute Convergence and Rearrangement

## Core Idea
A series ∑ aₙ converges absolutely if ∑ |aₙ| converges. Absolute convergence implies convergence. However, a conditionally convergent series (convergent but not absolutely) can be rearranged to converge to any real number or diverge, demonstrating that the order of terms matters for conditional convergence.
