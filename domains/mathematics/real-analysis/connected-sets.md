# Connected Sets

## Core Idea
A set is connected if it cannot be separated into two non-empty disjoint open subsets whose union is the whole set. On ℝ, the connected sets are precisely the intervals (of any type). Connectedness is essential for understanding why continuous images of connected sets are connected.
