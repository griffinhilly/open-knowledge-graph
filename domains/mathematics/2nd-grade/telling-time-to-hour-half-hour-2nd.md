# Telling Time to the Hour and Half Hour

## Core Idea
An analog clock has an hour hand (shorter) and minute hand (longer). When the minute hand points to 12, it's on the hour. When the minute hand points to 6, it's the half hour (30 minutes past). Understanding these two positions is foundational for more precise time reading.

## How It's Best Learned
Use large demonstration clocks that students can manipulate. Practice moving the hands to show specific times. Relate clock times to daily activities ('Lunch is at 12 o'clock'). Play 'show me the time' games.

## Common Misconceptions
- Confusing the hour and minute hands.
- Reading the hour hand when it's between numbers.
- Thinking the minute hand's position doesn't affect the hour reading.
