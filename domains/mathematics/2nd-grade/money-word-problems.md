# Money Word Problems

## Core Idea
Money word problems apply addition and subtraction skills in real-world shopping and spending contexts. Problems may ask: how much does it cost to buy two items? How much change is received? Can a purchase be made with a given amount? Students must identify the relevant quantities, choose the correct operation, compute accurately, and interpret the result in context.

## How It's Best Learned
Use familiar scenarios: school store, vending machine, toy shop. Require students to write an equation before computing. Discuss whether the answer is reasonable: 'If a book costs $4 and I have $3, can I buy it?' Extend to two-step problems once one-step is solid.

## Common Misconceptions
- Choosing addition when subtraction is needed (or vice versa) without reading carefully.
- Ignoring cents in the problem (treating $1.25 as 1).
- Not labeling the answer with the correct units (dollars vs. cents).
