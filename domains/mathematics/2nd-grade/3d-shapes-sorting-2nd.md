# Three-Dimensional Shapes and Sorting

## Core Idea
Three-dimensional shapes include cubes, rectangular prisms, cylinders, cones, and spheres. They have faces (flat surfaces), edges, and vertices. Sort 3D shapes by properties like whether they roll, stack, or have curved surfaces.
