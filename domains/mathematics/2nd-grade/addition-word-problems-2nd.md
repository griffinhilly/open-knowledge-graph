# Solving Addition Word Problems

## Core Idea
Addition word problems describe situations where quantities are combined or increased. Solving them involves identifying what the problem asks, deciding on the operation (addition), finding the answer, and checking if the answer makes sense.

## How It's Best Learned
Read problems aloud and discuss what's happening. Draw pictures or use manipulatives to represent the problem. Start with simple, one-step problems before progressing to more complex scenarios.

## Common Misconceptions
- Misidentifying the operation needed (adding when subtracting is required).
- Not reading the problem carefully enough.
- Getting the wrong answer but not checking if it's reasonable in context.
