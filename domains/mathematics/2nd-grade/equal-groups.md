# Equal Groups

## Core Idea
Equal groups are collections where every group has the same number of objects. Three groups of 4 apples each is a situation with equal groups: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12. Recognizing equal groups in pictures, stories, and real life is the conceptual foundation for multiplication. The total in an equal-groups situation can be found by repeated addition or skip counting.

## How It's Best Learned
Use physical objects to build equal groups before moving to pictures. Ask 'how many groups?' and 'how many in each group?' as two distinct questions. Connect to skip counting: 3 groups of 5 is just skipping by 5 three times. Make sure students can distinguish equal groups from unequal groups.

## Common Misconceptions
- Confusing 'groups of' with 'groups and' — students may add the number of groups to the group size.
- Not recognizing that equal-groups situations are different from plain addition.
- Thinking there is only one way to find the total (students should see both repeated addition and skip counting).
