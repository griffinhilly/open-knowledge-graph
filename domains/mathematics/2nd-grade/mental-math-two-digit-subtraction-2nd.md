# Mental Math Strategies for Two-Digit Subtraction

## Core Idea
Subtraction strategies include counting back by tens and ones, using related addition facts (thinking: what plus 13 equals 45?), or decomposing the subtracted number. Flexible strategies are faster than always regrouping.
