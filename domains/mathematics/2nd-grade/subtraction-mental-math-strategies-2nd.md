# Mental Math Strategies for Subtraction

## Core Idea
Subtraction strategies include: counting back (13 - 2: count 12, 11), counting up (13 - 5: start at 5, count 6, 7, 8, ..., 13), and using related addition facts (13 - 5: think 5 + 8 = 13, so 13 - 5 = 8). These strategies reveal the relationship between addition and subtraction.

## How It's Best Learned
Use a number line to model each strategy. Show how 'counting back' works for subtracting small amounts and 'counting up' works when the difference is small. Connect to addition facts.

## Common Misconceptions
- Counting backward without careful tracking.
- Not recognizing that addition and subtraction are related.
- Using inefficient strategies (like counting back by ones repeatedly).
