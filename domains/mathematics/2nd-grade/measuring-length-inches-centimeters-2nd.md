# Measuring Length: Inches and Centimeters

## Core Idea
Length can be measured in inches (customary) or centimeters (metric). An inch is about 2.5 centimeters. Accuracy in measurement requires carefully aligning the ruler's zero and reading to the correct mark.

## How It's Best Learned
Measure familiar classroom objects with both types of rulers. Notice that the same object has different numbers depending on the unit used. Estimate length before measuring to build number sense.

## Common Misconceptions
- Starting the measurement at the 1-inch or 1-cm mark instead of zero.
- Misreading between marks.
- Mixing units in a single measurement (saying '5 inches and 2 centimeters').
