# Even and Odd Numbers

## Core Idea
Even numbers can be divided into two equal groups with nothing left over; odd numbers always have one leftover. Numbers ending in 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 are even; numbers ending in 1, 3, 5, 7, or 9 are odd. The 'pairing' model gives students a concrete way to test any number, and the ones-digit rule provides a quick shortcut.

## How It's Best Learned
Have students pair physical objects and check for a leftover to determine odd or even. Connect to skip counting by 2s — the numbers reached are all even. Use the 'partner' metaphor: even means everyone has a partner, odd means one person is left out.

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking large numbers are always even.
- Forgetting that 0 is even.
- Applying the ones-digit rule without understanding that it works because of the base-ten structure.
