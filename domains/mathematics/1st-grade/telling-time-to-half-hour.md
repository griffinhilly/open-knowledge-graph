# Telling Time to the Half-Hour

## Core Idea
Telling time to the half-hour means reading times like 3:30, where the minute hand points to the 6 and 30 minutes have passed since the last full hour. On a digital clock, half-hour times end in :30. The hour hand sits halfway between two numbers at the half-hour, which requires recognizing it no longer points directly at a numeral.

## How It's Best Learned
Connect the half-hour to the fraction concept of one-half: the clock face has two halves, and at :30 the minute hand is halfway around. Color the two halves of the clock face in different colors to make this visual. Compare :00 and :30 positions of both hands side by side.

## Common Misconceptions
- Reading 3:30 as '3:6' because the minute hand points at the 6.
- Saying the hour is 4 when it is 3:30, because the hour hand is closer to 4.
- Not understanding that :30 always means 30 minutes past the named hour.
