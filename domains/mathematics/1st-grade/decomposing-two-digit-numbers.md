# Decomposing Two-Digit Numbers

## Core Idea
Students learn to break two-digit numbers into different parts—such as tens and ones, or other combinations—to make calculation easier. For example, 25 can be seen as 20 + 5, or as 15 + 10, or as 24 + 1. This flexibility with numbers is crucial for mental math.

## How It's Best Learned
Use visual models like number lines, base-10 blocks, and part-part-whole diagrams. Practice writing different equations that show different decompositions of the same number.
