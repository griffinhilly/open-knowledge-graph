# Composing Shapes

## Core Idea
Composing shapes means joining two or more shapes together along their edges to create a new, larger shape. Two right triangles joined at their longest sides form a rectangle; six equilateral triangles arranged around a center point form a hexagon. This spatial activity builds geometric intuition and foreshadows area concepts.

## How It's Best Learned
Use pattern blocks and tangrams so students can physically fit pieces together and feel the edges align. Start with guided tasks ('make a rectangle using exactly two triangles') before moving to open-ended creative challenges.

## Common Misconceptions
- Not aligning edges precisely, leaving gaps or overlaps.
- Difficulty predicting which shapes will fit together before physically trying.
- Losing track of the composed shape's identity once assembled from parts.
