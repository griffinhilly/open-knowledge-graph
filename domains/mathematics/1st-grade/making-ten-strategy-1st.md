# Making Ten Strategy

## Core Idea
To solve 8 + 5, break 5 into 2 + 3. Then 8 + 2 = 10, and 10 + 3 = 13. Using 10 as a benchmark makes the problem simpler. This strategy relies on knowing number bonds to 10 and is a bridge to place-value understanding.
