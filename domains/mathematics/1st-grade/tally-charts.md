# Tally Charts

## Core Idea
A tally chart records data by making one mark per item, with every fifth mark drawn diagonally across the previous four. The bundling into groups of five makes counting large totals fast and reduces errors. Tally charts are among the simplest tools for organizing collected data and comparing categories at a glance.

## How It's Best Learned
Have students collect real data in real time — favorite colors, types of pets, preferred snack — and record using tally marks as they count. Practice reading tallies by grouping the bundles of five first before adding any extras.

## Common Misconceptions
- Making the fifth tally mark vertical instead of diagonal across the group.
- Forgetting to bundle in fives and creating long unbroken rows.
- Counting tally marks one at a time rather than in groups of five.
