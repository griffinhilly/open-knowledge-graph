# Comparing Numbers 1 to 20

## Core Idea
Using the symbols <, >, and = to compare quantities: 12 > 8, 5 < 10, 7 = 7. Understanding 'greater than,' 'less than,' and 'equal to' supports logical reasoning and prepares for inequalities.
