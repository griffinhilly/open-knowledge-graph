# Subtraction Within 20

## Core Idea
Subtraction within 20 extends subtraction to larger numbers, with differences remaining at 20 or below. Students apply strategies like counting back, counting up to find a difference, and thinking addition (15 − 8 = ? becomes 8 + ? = 15). This fluency is essential before moving to multi-digit arithmetic.

## How It's Best Learned
Use ten-frames and number lines to visualize subtraction. Encourage 'think addition' as a primary strategy since most students find addition more natural. Relate each subtraction fact to its addition partner to reinforce the inverse relationship.

## Common Misconceptions
- Not connecting subtraction to addition, treating each operation in complete isolation.
- Counting back is error-prone for larger differences; encourage counting up instead.
- Mixing up the minuend and subtrahend, recording answers in the wrong order.
