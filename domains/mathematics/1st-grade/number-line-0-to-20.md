# Number Line (0 to 20)

## Core Idea
A number line is a straight line where equally spaced tick marks represent numbers in order, showing both sequence and distance. On a 0-to-20 number line, numbers increase from left to right. Students use number lines to model addition (jump right) and subtraction (jump left), making arithmetic operations spatial and visual.

## How It's Best Learned
Use a large floor number line where students physically hop to model addition and subtraction problems. Progress from physical hops to drawing arcs on paper number lines to eventually mental imagery. Emphasize that the spaces — not the tick marks — represent units.

## Common Misconceptions
- Counting the starting tick mark as one of the jumps, causing off-by-one errors.
- Placing numbers at unequal intervals because the line is not yet understood as a measurement tool.
- Jumping in the wrong direction for subtraction.
