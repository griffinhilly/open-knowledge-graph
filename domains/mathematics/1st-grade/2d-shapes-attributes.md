# Attributes of 2D Shapes

## Core Idea
Two-dimensional shapes are defined by their attributes: the number of sides, the number of corners (vertices), and whether sides are straight or curved. A triangle always has exactly 3 straight sides and 3 vertices; a rectangle has 4 sides and 4 right-angle corners. Focusing on defining attributes rather than overall appearance helps students correctly classify shapes in any size or orientation.

## How It's Best Learned
Have students trace sides with a finger and count corners by poking each vertex. Use yes/no attribute games: 'Is this a rectangle? How do you know?' Compare closely related shapes (squares vs. rectangles) to identify what attributes are required versus optional.

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking a tilted square is 'a diamond,' not a square (orientation confusion).
- Counting a circle's single curve as having 0 sides without understanding what defines a side.
- Confusing sides and vertices.
