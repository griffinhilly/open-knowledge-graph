# Skip Counting by 5s

## Core Idea
Skip counting by 5s means counting 5, 10, 15, 20, 25… by jumping five at a time. Every number in this sequence ends in 0 or 5, making the pattern easy to recognize and extend. This skill directly supports reading clock faces (minute marks) and counting nickels, giving it immediate real-world relevance.

## How It's Best Learned
Use fingers as a natural model — each full hand equals 5. Practice with nickel coins and clock faces. Color multiples of 5 on a hundred chart to see the diagonal stripe pattern emerge.

## Common Misconceptions
- Mixing up the rhythm of 5s and 2s after practicing both.
- Stopping too early when asked to count by 5s to a specific target number.
