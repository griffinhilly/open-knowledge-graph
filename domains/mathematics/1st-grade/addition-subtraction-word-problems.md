# Addition and Subtraction Word Problems

## Core Idea
Word problems present real-world situations requiring addition or subtraction to solve. The main problem types include joining (add-to), separating (take-from), comparing, and part-whole scenarios. Students must identify the unknown quantity — which might be the start, the change, or the result — choose the correct operation, and represent the situation with an equation.

## How It's Best Learned
Act out problems with physical objects before writing equations. Explicitly teach students to identify what is unknown rather than relying on keywords like 'total' or 'left,' which fail for many problem types. Rotate through all problem structures rather than always presenting the same format.

## Common Misconceptions
- Over-relying on keywords ('in all' = add, 'left' = subtract), which misclassifies many problems.
- Automatically adding both numbers in the problem without reading the situation.
- Difficulty with start-unknown problems (? + 5 = 12), where the unknown is not the final result.
