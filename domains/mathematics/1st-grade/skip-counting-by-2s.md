# Skip Counting by 2s

## Core Idea
Skip counting by 2s means counting 2, 4, 6, 8, 10… by jumping over every other number. This skill connects counting to multiplication and reveals the concept of even numbers. Recognizing that every number in this sequence ends in 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 helps build number sense and mental math fluency.

## How It's Best Learned
Use rhythmic counting with clapping on each spoken number and whispering skipped numbers. Show the pattern on a number line or hundred chart by coloring every other square. Pair objects to connect skip counting to the idea of pairs.

## Common Misconceptions
- Losing the rhythm and reverting to counting by 1s partway through.
- Not recognizing that skip counting by 2s always produces even numbers.
