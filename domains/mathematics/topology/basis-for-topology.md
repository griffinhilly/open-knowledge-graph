# Basis for a Topology

## Core Idea
A basis 𝔅 is a collection of open sets such that every open set is a union of basis elements. Specifying a basis is more efficient than listing all open sets. A collection is a basis iff it covers X and for any B₁, B₂ ∈ 𝔅, their intersection is a union of basis elements. The basis uniquely generates a topology.
