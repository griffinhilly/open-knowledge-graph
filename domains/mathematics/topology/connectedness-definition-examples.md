# Connectedness: Definition and Examples

## Core Idea
A space is connected if it cannot be split into two disjoint nonempty open sets. Equivalently, the only subsets that are both open and closed are the empty set and the whole space. Connectedness captures the intuition that a space 'hangs together' and is fundamental to understanding global structure.
