# The Fundamental Group

## Core Idea
The fundamental group π₁(X, x₀) is the group of homotopy classes of loops based at x₀, with concatenation as operation. It measures 'holes' in a space and is a topological invariant. Homeomorphic spaces have isomorphic fundamental groups, making it a primary algebraic tool for distinguishing spaces.
