# Fundamental Group of the Circle

## Core Idea
The fundamental group of the circle S¹ is isomorphic to ℤ, where the isomorphism is given by the winding number (how many times a loop winds around the circle). This canonical example shows that topological properties can be captured by group invariants and demonstrates the power of algebraic topology.
