# Homotopy of Paths

## Core Idea
Two paths from x to y are homotopic if one continuously deforms into the other while keeping endpoints fixed. Homotopy is an equivalence relation on paths; homotopy classes form the fundamental group. Path homotopy captures topological equivalence of paths.
