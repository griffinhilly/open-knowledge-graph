# Completeness of Metric Spaces

## Core Idea
A metric space is complete if every Cauchy sequence converges. ℝⁿ and ℓᵖ are complete; ℚ and (0,1) are not. Complete spaces behave well: compact metric spaces are complete, closed subsets of complete spaces are complete. Incompleteness causes 'missing limits'; completion adds points to make all Cauchy sequences converge.
