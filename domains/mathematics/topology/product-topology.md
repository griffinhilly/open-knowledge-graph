# Product Topology

## Core Idea
The product topology on ∏Xᵢ is generated by the basis of all ∏Uᵢ where each Uᵢ is open and only finitely many differ from Xᵢ. This is the coarsest topology making all projections continuous. Products of compact spaces remain compact (Tychonoff's theorem).
