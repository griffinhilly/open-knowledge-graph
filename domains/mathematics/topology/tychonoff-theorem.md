# Tychonoff's Theorem

## Core Idea
Tychonoff's theorem states that arbitrary products of compact spaces are compact—this is a deep result requiring the Axiom of Choice. It extends the finite product result and is essential for many applications in functional analysis and topology. The theorem demonstrates the power of topological generalization beyond metric spaces.
