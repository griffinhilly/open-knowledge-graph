# Divisibility Theory (Formal Treatment)

## Core Idea
Divisibility theory formalizes the divisibility relation on integers and explores its properties systematically. It establishes the foundation for all of number theory by defining divisors, exploring the structure of divisibility relations, and connecting to other algebraic concepts. This formal treatment goes beyond intuitive understanding to rigorous proof.
