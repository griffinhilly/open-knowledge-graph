# Best Rational Approximations and Continued Fractions

## Core Idea
The convergents p_n/q_n of a continued fraction expansion provide best rational approximations in a strong sense: the n-th convergent minimizes |x − p/q| over all fractions with denominator at most q_n. This connects approximation theory to the structure of number expansions.
