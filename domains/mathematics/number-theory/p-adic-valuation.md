# p-adic Valuation

## Core Idea
The p-adic valuation v_p(n) measures the highest power of prime p dividing n. It satisfies properties analogous to absolute value, v_p(mn) = v_p(m) + v_p(n), but gives a different metric where numbers are 'close' if their difference is highly divisible by p.
