# Product Measures

## Core Idea
Given measure spaces (X, ℱ, μ) and (Y, 𝒢, ν), the product measure space (X × Y, ℱ ⊗ 𝒢, μ ⊗ ν) is defined on the product σ-algebra generated by measurable rectangles A × B, with (μ ⊗ ν)(A × B) = μ(A)ν(B).
