# Orthogonality in Hilbert Spaces

## Core Idea
Elements u, v are orthogonal if ⟨u, v⟩ = 0. The orthogonal complement M⊥ of a closed subspace M is always closed and satisfies H = M ⊕ M⊥, enabling orthogonal decompositions.
