# Weak* Convergence

## Core Idea
A sequence of functionals (fₙ) in X* converges weak* to f if fₙ(x) → f(x) for every x ∈ X. The weak* topology on X* enables compactness: the closed unit ball is weak* compact (Alaoglu's theorem).
