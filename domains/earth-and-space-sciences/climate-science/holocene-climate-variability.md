# Holocene Climate Variability

## Core Idea
The Holocene (past 11,700 years) was the current interglacial and generally stable relative to glacial periods, but experienced significant variability. The Early Holocene was marked by millennial-scale oscillations; the Mid-Holocene Optimum (6,000–9,000 years ago) was warm in the Northern Hemisphere; and the Late Holocene cooled toward the preindustrial period. Natural variability during the Holocene helps contextualize the rapid anthropogenic warming of recent decades.
