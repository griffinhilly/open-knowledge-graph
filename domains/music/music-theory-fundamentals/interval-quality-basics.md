# Interval Quality Basics

## Core Idea
After counting the generic interval name, interval quality describes whether it's perfect, major, minor, augmented, or diminished. Perfect intervals (unison, 4th, 5th, octave) are either perfect or altered. Major intervals (2nd, 3rd, 6th, 7th) are major, minor, or altered. Quality is determined by comparing the interval to the major scale.

## How It's Best Learned
Build intervals from the major scale pattern, identifying each as major or perfect. Practice recognizing intervals by ear. Build intervals on keyboard to understand semitone relationships.

## Common Misconceptions
Thinking all intervals come in major and minor (perfect intervals don't). Confusing harmonic and melodic intervals regarding quality. Miscounting semitones when determining quality.
