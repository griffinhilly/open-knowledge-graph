# Cadence Types and Their Functions

## Core Idea
A cadence is a harmonic formula that concludes a phrase or section. The authentic cadence (V–I) provides strong closure and resolution. The plagal cadence (IV–I) offers gentler resolution. The half cadence (ending on V) leaves the music suspended. The deceptive cadence (V–vi) surprises by avoiding expected resolution. Cadences structure musical form and clarify phrase boundaries.

## How It's Best Learned
Listen to phrases in classical and contemporary music and identify their cadences. Compose short phrases with different cadence types.

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking the V–I progression is the only authentic cadence (V7–I is also authentic).
- Not recognizing that cadences involve more than just the final two chords; the approach and voice leading matter significantly.
