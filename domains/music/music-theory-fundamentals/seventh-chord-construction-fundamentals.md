# Seventh Chord Construction: Types and Qualities

## Core Idea
Seventh chords extend triads by adding a seventh above the root. The five common seventh chord types are major seventh (major triad + major seventh), dominant seventh (major triad + minor seventh), minor seventh (minor triad + minor seventh), half-diminished (diminished triad + minor seventh), and fully diminished (diminished triad + diminished seventh).

## How It's Best Learned
Build seventh chords on a staff starting from major and minor triads. Play and listen to each type to internalize their distinct qualities and harmonic functions.

## Common Misconceptions
A dominant seventh is not built from the dominant scale—it is a major triad built on scale degree V plus a minor seventh above the root, creating tritone tension.
