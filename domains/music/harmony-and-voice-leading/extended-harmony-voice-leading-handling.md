# Extended Harmony: Voice Leading with 9ths, 11ths, and 13ths

## Core Idea
Extended chords containing ninths, elevenths, and thirteenths require special voice-leading attention because upper extensions must resolve like non-harmonic tones or be prepared smoothly. The tritone may appear in unexpected positions. Understanding which extensions are consonant or dissonant guides voice-leading decisions and chord voicing choices.
