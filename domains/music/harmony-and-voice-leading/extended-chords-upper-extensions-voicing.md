# Extended Chords and Upper Extensions in Voice Leading

## Core Idea
Extended chords add ninths, elevenths, and thirteenths above the seventh, expanding harmonic color and linear possibilities. Voice-leading with extended chords requires understanding which extensions can be doubled, how alterations (raised/lowered extensions) affect resolution, and whether extensions function as chord tones or passing/embellishing tones. Careful handling of upper extensions prevents harmonic ambiguity while enriching harmonic vocabulary.
