# Rondo and Rounded Binary Form Design

## Core Idea
Rondo form (A-B-A-C-A or similar patterns) alternates a stable refrain with contrasting episodes, creating a sense of return and unity. Rounded binary adds a return to the A section after the B section, bridging binary and ternary forms. Both forms are effective for clear, memorable structure.
