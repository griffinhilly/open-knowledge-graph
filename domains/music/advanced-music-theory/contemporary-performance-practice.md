# Performance Practice in Contemporary and New Music

## Core Idea
Contemporary music requires performers to make interpretive choices that traditional notation leaves unspecified: how to execute extended techniques, balance between precision and flexibility, and realize composer intent while accommodating acoustic realities. Understanding performance practice is essential for both composers and analysts.
