# Advanced Jazz Reharmonization and Chord Substitution

## Core Idea
Advanced reharmonization extends basic substitution (tritone, relative minor) to include tensions, altered scales, and non-functional harmony. Reharmonization becomes a vehicle for melodic reinterpretation and soloist interaction, where harmonic support reveals new aspects of a familiar tune.
