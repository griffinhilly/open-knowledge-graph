# Performing with Expression

## Core Idea
Performing with expression means using dynamics, tempo, and phrasing to bring music to life. Playing or singing the right notes is just the beginning; how you shape those notes with louder and softer moments, faster and slower sections, and smooth or detached articulation is what makes a performance musical.

## How It's Best Learned
Take a song the class already knows well and experiment with performing it different ways: whisper-soft, bold and loud, with a dramatic slow section. Record two versions and compare which one feels more expressive. Discuss what specific choices made the difference.

## Common Misconceptions
- Thinking expression means exaggerating everything; subtle changes are often more effective than dramatic ones.
- Believing expression is something only advanced performers do; even beginners can play a note louder or softer.
- Assuming there is one correct way to perform a piece expressively; different performers bring their own interpretation.

## Explainer

Expression in music means performing in a way that shows feeling and emotion. When you perform with expression you use your voice your instrument your body language and your phrasing to create a performance that touches people is hearts. The same song can sound sad happy excited gentle or dramatic depending on how you perform it with expression.
